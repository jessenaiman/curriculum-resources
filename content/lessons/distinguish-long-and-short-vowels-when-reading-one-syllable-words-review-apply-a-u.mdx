## Try
- Title: Decode and Sort on the Board
- Description: Display one-syllable words, model the first comparison, then invite students to read each word aloud and sort it into a long- or short-vowel column.
- Tip: Check accurate decoding before categorizing. Note whether an error comes from the spelling pattern, decoding, or vowel identification.

## Check
- Title: Five-Word Quick Check
- Description: Present five previously untaught one-syllable words that use familiar patterns. Students read each word and identify the vowel as long or short.
- Tip: Separate pattern gaps from general decoding difficulty before deciding whether to move on or reteach.

## Planning note
Grade 2 applies the long- and short-vowel distinction in print. The linked UFLI materials cover the VCe subset, so use them only for patterns students have already learned and supplement them when the lesson target includes other vowel spellings.
