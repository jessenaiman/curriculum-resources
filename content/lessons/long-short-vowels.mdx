## Grade 1
- Lesson: Lesson 1
- Mode: Oral — no print
- Standards: US RF.1.2.a · Ontario B2.1 alignment
- Goal: Distinguish long from short vowel sounds in spoken single-syllable words
- Materials: Two response cards marked “long” and “short”

### Introduce: Long vs. Short Sound Sort
- Teacher: Say familiar single-syllable words aloud, emphasizing the vowel without showing print.
- Students: Clap once for a short vowel sound and twice for a long vowel sound.
- Look for: Students respond to the sound they hear rather than a spelling they remember.
- Resource state: none
- Resource note: This is a teacher-led oral routine. The tracker supplies a UFLI toolbox link, but it does not identify one ready-to-use resource that teaches this complete contrast.

### Teach together: Teacher-Led Sound Discrimination
- Teacher: Say 8–10 contrasting words. Repeat or stretch the vowel when students need another listen.
- Students: Hold up a “long” or “short” card and explain what they heard.
- Look for: Consistent identification across more than one vowel; note repeated confusions.
- Resource state: none
- Resource note: Teacher-led; no external resource is required.

### Student practice: Partner Listening Check
- Teacher: Say eight familiar words one at a time and pause after each response.
- Students: Show the “long” or “short” response card, then compare their answer with a partner.
- Look for: Students classify the spoken vowel sound without relying on printed words or pictures.
- Resource state: none
- Resource note: Teacher-led; no picture-card resource is required.

### Check understanding: Quick Oral Exit
- Teacher: Say five new words one at a time.
- Students: Respond “long” or “short” aloud.
- Look for: Four or five correct responses; repeated errors identify the vowel contrast to reteach.
- Resource state: none
- Resource note: Teacher-led; no external resource is needed.

## Grade 2
- Lesson: Lesson 17
- Mode: Reading — print-based review
- Standards: US RF.2.3.a · Ontario B2.1–B2.3 alignment
- Goal: Distinguish long and short vowels when reading one-syllable words
- Materials: Displayed word cards or board; five-word exit check

### Introduce: Read-Aloud — Spot the Long Vowel
- Teacher: Read a short passage or word list containing familiar long- and short-vowel patterns.
- Students: Raise a hand when they hear a long-vowel word and identify the word.
- Look for: Students connect the heard vowel sound to the printed word during review.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: UFLI Foundations Lesson 59 — VCe Review 2 Slides
- Source: University of Florida Literacy Institute
- URL: https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/59_Slides_UFLIFoundations.pptx
- Resource role: Explicit review of final-e long-vowel patterns
- Resource note: Exact slide deck verified. It supports CVC/VCe contrasts but does not represent every long-vowel spelling.

### Teach together: Decode & Sort on the Board
- Teacher: Display one-syllable words, model the first comparison, then invite students to lead the sort.
- Students: Read each word aloud and sort it into a long- or short-vowel column.
- Look for: Accurate decoding before categorizing; note whether errors come from decoding or vowel identification.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: UFLI Foundations Lesson 59 — VCe Review 2 Slides
- Source: University of Florida Literacy Institute
- URL: https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/59_Slides_UFLIFoundations.pptx
- Resource role: Guided CVC/VCe reading and word work
- Resource note: Exact slide deck verified; use only after students have learned the relevant final-e patterns.

### Student practice: Vowel Sort Worksheet
- Teacher: Assign the UFLI practice that best matches the patterns already taught.
- Students: Read printed words and sort them into long- and short-vowel groups.
- Look for: Students read each word rather than sorting by visual guesswork.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: UFLI Foundations Lesson 59 — Home Practice
- Source: University of Florida Literacy Institute
- URL: https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/59_HomePractice_UFLI-Foundations.pdf
- Resource role: Independent or partner practice
- Resource note: Exact PDF verified. It practises final-e long-vowel patterns and should follow explicit instruction.

### Check understanding: Five-Word Quick Check
- Teacher: Present five previously untaught one-syllable words that use familiar patterns.
- Students: Read each word and identify the vowel as long or short.
- Look for: Accurate decoding and classification; separate pattern gaps from general decoding difficulty.
- Resource state: none
- Resource note: Teacher-led; use the quick check to decide whether to proceed or reteach.
