## Grade 1
- Lesson: Mathematics sequence 1
- Mode: Concrete models — within 20
- Standards: US 1.OA.A.1 · Ontario B2
- Goal: Represent and solve addition and subtraction word problems within 20
- Materials: Counters or Number Frames; board or chart paper

### Introduce: Act Out the Story
- Teacher: Tell a short join or separate story with quantities students can act out using counters.
- Students: Build the starting quantity, change the model as the story changes, and state what must be found.
- Look for: Students represent the action in the story before naming addition or subtraction.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: Number Frames
- Source: The Math Learning Center
- URL: https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps/number-frames
- Resource role: Concrete model for all four lesson steps
- Resource note: Workbook-recommended source for representing quantities and changes.

### Teach together: Model, Draw, and Write
- Teacher: Model one problem with a number frame, sketch the model, and connect it to an equation.
- Students: Explain what each number represents and solve a second example with the teacher.
- Look for: The equation matches the story rather than a keyword guess.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: Number Frames
- Source: The Math Learning Center
- URL: https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps/number-frames
- Resource role: Shared modelling workspace
- Resource note: Keep totals and differences within 20; Ontario fact fluency expectations remain narrower.

### Student practice: Solve and Explain Two Stories
- Teacher: Provide one joining and one separating story with space for a model, equation, and answer sentence.
- Students: Build or draw each problem, write an equation, and explain how the model matches the story.
- Look for: Students distinguish the known quantities from the unknown and can justify the operation.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: K5 Learning — Mixed Addition & Subtraction Word Problems (Grade 1)
- Source: K5 Learning
- URL: https://www.k5learning.com/worksheets/math/grade-1-addition-subtraction-word-problems-single-digits-1.pdf
- Resource role: Exact practice worksheet with one- and two-step problems within 20
- Resource note: Direct three-page PDF verified; the final page contains answers.

### Check understanding: One New Story
- Teacher: Read one unfamiliar one-step problem and ask for a model plus an equation.
- Students: Solve independently, then explain what the answer means in the story.
- Look for: The model, equation, and answer sentence agree.
- Resource state: none
- Resource note: Teacher-led; use a new problem rather than repeating a guided example.

## Grade 2
- Lesson: Mathematics sequence 7
- Mode: One- and two-step reasoning — Ontario core within 50
- Standards: US 2.OA.A.1 · Ontario B2 alignment described in workbook notes
- Goal: Solve one- and two-step addition and subtraction word problems
- Materials: Number Pieces or counters; board or chart paper

### Introduce: What Happens First?
- Teacher: Present a two-part story and pause after the first change. Ask what is known now and what happens next.
- Students: Retell the story in order and model each change.
- Look for: Students separate the two steps instead of combining unrelated numbers immediately.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: Number Pieces
- Source: The Math Learning Center
- URL: https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps/number-pieces
- Resource role: Place-value model for all four lesson steps
- Resource note: The workbook recommends Number Pieces for the Grade 2 row.

### Teach together: Organize a Two-Step Solution
- Teacher: Record the first equation, label its result, and use that result in the second equation.
- Students: Explain why the first answer becomes information for the next step.
- Look for: Students preserve the meaning of the intermediate result.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: Number Pieces
- Source: The Math Learning Center
- URL: https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps/number-pieces
- Resource role: Shared modelling workspace
- Resource note: For Ontario core instruction, keep the number range within 50; within 100 is a US-aligned extension.

### Student practice: Model, Solve, and Check
- Teacher: Provide one one-step and one two-step story. Ask students to estimate whether each answer is reasonable.
- Students: Use a model or drawing, write the equation sequence, and check the result against the story.
- Look for: Correct sequence, reasonable intermediate value, and an answer that responds to the question asked.
- Resource state: ready
- Resource: K5 Learning — Mixed Addition & Subtraction Within 20 (Grade 2)
- Source: K5 Learning
- URL: https://www.k5learning.com/worksheets/math/grade-2-word-problems-mixed-add-subtract-d.pdf
- Resource role: Exact practice worksheet with multi-step reasoning
- Resource note: Direct PDF verified. It stays within 20, which is narrower than the Ontario Grade 2 core range, and is appropriate as an entry task or diagnostic.

### Check understanding: Explain the Two Steps
- Teacher: Give one short two-step problem and ask students to label what each equation finds.
- Students: Solve and add a phrase beside each equation explaining its purpose.
- Look for: Students understand both operations and do not merely produce an unexplained answer.
- Resource state: none
- Resource note: Teacher-led; diagnostic explanation is more useful than an additional worksheet here.

## Planning note
The workbook’s Grade 1 row aligns well when work stays within 20. Its Grade 2 source row uses the US range within 100, while Ontario Grade 2 core operations are generally within 50. Keep the reasoning and one-/two-step structure, but adjust the number range before treating the lesson as Ontario core instruction.
