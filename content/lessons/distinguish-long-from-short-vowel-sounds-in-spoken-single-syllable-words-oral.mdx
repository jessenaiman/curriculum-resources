## Try
- Title: Long vs. Short Sound Sort
- Description: Say familiar single-syllable words aloud, emphasizing the vowel without showing print. Students clap once for a short vowel and twice for a long vowel.
- Tip: Use familiar words first, then repeat the contrast with new words. Listen for whether the child responds to the sound rather than a remembered spelling.

## Practice
- Title: Partner Listening Check
- Description: Say eight familiar words one at a time. Students show a long or short response card, then compare their answer with a partner.
- Printable: false
- Icons: response-cards, listening, partner-talk

## Check
- Title: Quick Oral Exit
- Description: Say five new words one at a time. Students respond long or short aloud without seeing the words.
- Tip: Four or five correct responses is a useful starting signal. Repeated errors identify the vowel contrast to reteach.
