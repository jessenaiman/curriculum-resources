# Miss Hayley

> Staff character card content for design transfer. Source: `Staff/Miss Hayley.png`.

## Role

- **Role:** Classroom teacher; movement, music and storytelling
- **Species:** Human adult female puppet
- **Setting:** Grade 1/2 classroom, stories, drama and creative movement

## Personality and strengths

- Warm, enthusiastic and welcoming
- Creative storyteller and singer
- Encourages movement and expression
- Builds confidence in every child
- Patient, kind and inspiring
- Leads with empathy and positivity
- Makes learning fun and memorable

## Identity lock - must preserve

- Female human adult puppet
- Friendly face with big eyes and smile
- Medium-brown wavy hair with dark band under a brown cowboy hat
- Red plaid shirt
- Blue denim overalls with front pocket
- Brown lace-up boots with rolled cuffs
- Felt-and-fabric puppet construction
- Warm, approachable teacher presence

## Friends and collaborators

- **Old MacDonald:** founder and fellow teacher; school leader
- **Miss Puddles:** music and drama teacher; creative partner
- **Mr Rusty:** music and dance teacher; activity partner
- **Mr Sam:** math and building teacher; classroom collaborator

## On-screen use

- Singing songs with students
- Telling stories and reading aloud
- Leading circle time and discussions
- Guiding pretend play and imagination
- Supporting class performances
- Running art, music and drama activities

## Wardrobe and props

**Clothing:** brown cowboy hat; red plaid shirt; blue denim overalls; brown lace-up boots.

**Props:** storybook; puppet theater; ukulele or guitar; art supplies; maracas.

## Production reference

Create Miss Hayley as a handcrafted felt-and-fabric puppet in the style of Old MacDonald's Farm School. Female human adult with friendly face, big eyes, warm smile and medium-brown wavy hair under a brown cowboy hat with dark band. She wears a red plaid shirt, blue denim overalls with front pocket and rolled cuffs, plus brown lace-up boots. Felt texture with visible stitching throughout. Warm, approachable teacher presence. Full body, neutral standing pose with one hand raised. Use for singing, storytelling, circle time, pretend play, class performances, reading aloud and art/music/drama activities.
