# Old MacDonald

> Staff character card content for design transfer. Source: `Staff/Old MacDonald.png`.

## Role

- **Role:** Farm School founder, teacher, storyteller and music maker
- **Species:** Elderly human male puppet
- **Setting:** Whole school; assemblies, stories and music

## Personality and strengths

- Warm, kind and welcoming
- Encouraging and patient
- Loves music, stories and learning
- Connects people and animals
- Builds community and togetherness
- Leads with heart and experience

## Identity lock - must preserve

- Elderly male puppet with full white hair, beard and moustache
- Straw hat with a dark band
- Red plaid shirt and blue denim overalls
- Brown boots
- Banjo on his shoulder with strap
- Friendly eyes and smiling expression
- Felt texture and stitched details

## Friends and collaborators

- **Miss Hayley:** classroom leader and activity partner
- **Miss Puddles:** music and drama collaborator
- **Mr Rusty:** farm projects and outdoor activities
- **Mr Sam:** math and building teacher

Together they teach, sing, learn and create on the farm.

## On-screen use

- Leads songs and music-making
- Reads stories and shares farm tales
- Hosts school gatherings and celebrations
- Welcomes students and families
- Teaches life lessons through play and song
- Appears in music numbers and movement breaks
- Participates in full-cast scenes and activities

## Wardrobe and props

**Clothing:** straw hat with dark band; red plaid shirt; blue denim overalls; brown boots.

**Props:** banjo with strap; school book with apple, A block and music note; optional guitar for songs; Farm School sign; music and teaching props as needed.

## Production reference

Create Old MacDonald as a handcrafted felt-and-fabric puppet in the Old MacDonald's Farm School style. Elderly male with white hair, full white beard and moustache, friendly eyes and smiling expression. Straw hat with dark band, red plaid shirt, blue denim overalls and brown boots. Banjo on shoulder with strap. Preserve felt texture, visible stitching, soft proportions and warm, welcoming posture for educational farm-school settings.
