# Miss Puddles

> Staff character card content for design transfer. Source: `Staff/Miss Puddles.png`.

## Role

- **Role:** Daycare teacher
- **Species:** Yellow duck adult
- **Setting:** Daycare circle time, fingerplays, early art and gentle transitions

## Personality and strengths

- Nurturing and patient
- Calm and dependable
- Loves songs and fingerplays
- Practical and organized
- Enjoys art and crafts
- Clear and gentle communicator
- Creates safe, happy spaces

## Ambitions

- Help every child feel safe and included
- Lead joyful songs, stories and fingerplays
- Inspire creativity with art and crafts
- Encourage sharing, kindness and teamwork
- Plan fun, hands-on learning each day

## Friends and collaborators

- **Old MacDonald:** morning gathering
- **Miss Hayley:** human teacher
- **Mr Rusty:** music and dance teacher
- **Mr Sam:** daycare art props

## Identity lock - must preserve

- Yellow duck puppet with open, cheerful beak
- Blue-and-white gingham dress
- Blue polka-dot bandana tied in a bow on the head
- Silver whistle on a cord
- Carries a rolled blue blanket
- Warm, nurturing demeanor with low, child-level posture
- Works with art, songs, circle time and soft materials
- Felt/fabric texture with stitched details

## Performance notes

- Low posture; stay at eye level with children
- Use gentle movements and patient pacing
- Use songs, fingerplays and rhythms
- Use simple, clear language
- Guide circle time and transitions
- Encourage creativity and choices

## Wardrobe and props

**Clothing:** blue-and-white gingham dress; blue polka-dot bandana; silver whistle on cord; rolled blue blanket.

**Props:** whistle; rolled blanket; paintbrush; pencil cup; storybook; colored papers; blocks; yarn basket.

## Production reference

Create Miss Puddles as a handcrafted needle-felt and fabric puppet in the style of Old MacDonald's Farm School. Yellow duck with soft curly felt texture, orange beak and webbed feet, black round eyes with lashes and eyebrows. Blue-and-white gingham dress with stitched seams. Blue polka-dot bandana tied in a bow on her head. Silver metal whistle on a gray cord around the neck. Carries a rolled blue blanket tucked under her arm. Soft, rounded puppet proportions with low posture for child-level interaction. Warm, friendly materials with visible stitching. Daycare teacher aesthetic.
