# Mr Sam

> Staff character card content for design transfer. Source: `Staff/Mr Sam.png`.

## Role

- **Role:** Math and building teacher
- **Species:** Pink pig adult
- **Setting:** Grade 1/2 counting, measuring, making and simple engineering

## Personality and strengths

- Inventive and patient
- Calm thinker and problem-solver
- Hands-on and practical
- Loves counting and measuring
- Explains steps clearly
- Finds solutions
- Encourages teamwork
- Leads hands-on activities

## Ambitions

- Build and design things that work
- Help children understand math in fun, hands-on ways
- Create a science and discovery table
- Make the classroom a maker space
- Inspire curiosity and problem-solving

## Friends and collaborators

- **Old MacDonald:** morning gathering
- **Miss Puddles:** music and drama
- **Miss Hayley:** classroom leader
- **Mr Rusty:** build projects

## Identity lock - must preserve

- Pink pig with round snout, soft ears and upright ears
- Round black glasses
- Red polka-dot bow tie
- Green quilted vest with gold buttons, pocket watch and pencils
- Cream shirt
- Brown felt trousers and brown hooves
- Felt texture and soft stitched details
- Gentle smile and thoughtful expression
- Calm, patient manner
- Carries a blue book and uses tools in class

## Performance notes

- Uses a calm thinking pose, such as hand to chin
- Shows the sequence: plan, build, test, improve
- Counts and measures out loud
- Demonstrates with tools and models
- Encourages and supports each child
- Celebrates solutions and effort

## Wardrobe and props

**Clothing:** round black glasses; red polka-dot bow tie; green quilted vest with pocket watch and pencils; cream shirt; brown trousers.

**Props:** blue book; pencil cup; ruler; blocks; hammer; saw; tape measure; balance scale.
