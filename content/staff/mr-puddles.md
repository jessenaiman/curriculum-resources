# Mr Puddles

> Staff character card content for design transfer. Source: `Staff/Mr Puddles.png`.

## Role

- **Role:** Art and photography teacher
- **Species:** Pale-yellow duck adult
- **Setting:** Whole school nature studies, art, school events and exhibitions

## Quick read

- Curious, gentle and observant
- Bird watcher, painter and photographer
- His art keeps turning into bird studies

## Identity lock - must preserve

- Pale-yellow duck adult
- Orange bill and feet
- Brown checkered cap
- Blue vest
- Handcrafted felt-and-fabric puppet construction
- Friendly eyes
- Compact adult proportions

## Staff friends and collaborators

- **Miss Puddles:** family scenes and daycare visits
- **Old MacDonald:** school events and photo moments
- **Miss Maisy:** bulletin boards and displays

## On-screen use

- Photographs school events and nature finds
- Paints bird pictures and sketches nests and feathers
- Carries a camera, sketchbook or paint kit
- Fits quiet observation scenes and art-table lessons

## Wardrobe and props

**Clothing:** brown checkered cap; blue vest.

**Props:** camera; binoculars; sketchbook; bird paintings; paintbrush; field notebook; watercolor palette.

## Production reference

Mr Puddles is a handcrafted felt-and-fabric puppet in the Old MacDonald's Farm School style. He is a pale-yellow duck adult with an orange bill and feet, wearing a brown checkered cap and blue vest. Preserve his compact adult proportions, friendly eyes and soft stitched details. Maintain the cozy, handcrafted school world. He frequently appears with art and nature props such as a camera, sketchbook, binoculars, paintbrushes and bird paintings. Keep his expression warm, curious and cheerful in all scenes.
