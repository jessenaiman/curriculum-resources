# Mr Rusty

> Staff character card content for design transfer. Source: `Staff/Mr Rusty.png`.

## Role

- **Role:** Music and dance teacher
- **Species:** Brown horse adult
- **Setting:** Kindergarten-Grade 1/2 rhythm, fiddle, barn-dance and movement scenes

## Personality and strengths

- Warm and encouraging
- Rhythmic and steady
- Loves music and singing
- Keeps a steady beat
- Leads barn-dance steps
- Brings energy with movement
- Helps others join in

## Ambitions

- Lead fun songs and dances
- Teach simple clap patterns and steps
- Encourage everyone to keep the beat
- Play the fiddle for gatherings
- Make music a joyful part of every day

## Friends and collaborators

- **Old MacDonald:** morning gathering
- **Miss Puddles:** music and drama
- **Miss Hayley:** class plays and movement
- **Mr Sam:** daycare art props

## Identity lock - must preserve

- Brown horse puppet with dark mane and tail
- Blue bandana tied at the neck
- Dark vest, cream shirt and brown pants
- Always seen with a fiddle and bow
- Warm smile and encouraging expression
- Uses music and rhythm to lead
- Calm, grounded posture with movement and dance
- Handcrafted felt-and-fabric puppet construction

## Performance notes

- Plays fiddle while standing or stepping
- Uses clear bow strokes and steady rhythm
- Leads barn-dance steps: side, step, turn, clap
- Claps patterns to cue group participation
- Encourages singing with eye contact
- Keeps energy warm, steady and inclusive

## Wardrobe and props

**Clothing:** blue bandana; dark brown vest; cream shirt; brown pants; fiddle and bow.

**Props:** fiddle and bow; music-note book; rhythm stick; tambourine; music-note box; horseshoes.

## Production reference

Create Mr Rusty as a handcrafted needle-felt and fabric puppet in the style of Old MacDonald's Farm School. Brown horse with dark brown yarn mane and tail, warm smile, black button eyes and felt muzzle. Blue bandana tied at the neck, dark brown felt vest, cream felt shirt and brown felt pants with rolled cuffs; dark felt hoof mitts or boots. Violin/fiddle and bow are wooden/felt props sized for puppet hands. Textures should show visible stitching and soft felt fibers. Pose: playing fiddle with bow, with one leg slightly lifted in a dance step. Handcrafted, sewn, textile details, soft wool fibers, children's television puppet aesthetic.
