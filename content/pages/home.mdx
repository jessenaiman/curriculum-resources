## Plan from the lesson, not a catalogue
This site organizes resources in the order a teacher actually uses them: introduce the idea, teach it together, let students practise, and check what they understood.

## Know what to use
Each topic begins with one selected resource and tells you exactly where it fits. You are not asked to compare ten nearly identical links.

## Know how to use it
Teacher and student actions are separated so a new teacher can understand the lesson quickly while an experienced teacher can scan it.

## Know where to look next
If the selected resource does not fit your class, targeted search prompts help you find a worksheet, demonstration, visual, or intervention without starting from an empty search box.
