Storytime Songs

Sing along with YDL storytime presenters to some of our favorite songs we use at the library! Stand up and do the actions to build large motor skills. Practice the finger plays to improve small motor skills. Singing and movement are important early learning activities to help get your little one ready for kindergarten.
