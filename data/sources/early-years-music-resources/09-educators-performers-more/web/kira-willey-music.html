Here's What I've Learned

Music immediately, fully engages kids, body and mind.

Music bypasses all the resistance that comes with “Now we’re going to practice mindfulness.” Kids hear a song, and they’re in. Bodies moving. Voices singing. Feelings shifting.

That’s not magic—it’s neuroscience. Music activates multiple parts of the brain at once. It regulates the nervous system. It creates connection.

And the best part? You don’t have to do anything except press play.

These songs were created for real moments—car rides, bedtime, dance parties, transitions, big feelings. Wherever your family is, there’s a song for it.

Here's how this music fits into your real life...

Morning Routines

Start the Day with Energy

Play an upbeat song while getting dressed. Let your kid dance while brushing teeth. Turn “time to go” into a game with music. Pick one “morning song” and play it every day. Watch how much easier transitions become when there’s a soundtrack.

Car Rides

Backseat Sanity-Savers

Turn “Are we there yet?” into sing-alongs. These songs shift car ride energy—calming restless bodies, brightening cranky moods, and making the drive feel better for everyone.

Bedtime Wind-down

Songs That Calm and Soothe

Gentle, dreamy songs that settle overstimulated nervous systems and create the peaceful atmosphere bedtime needs. Not background noise—intentional soundtracks that prepare bodies and minds for sleep.

Big Feelings & Resets

When Things Are Falling Apart

Big Feelings taking over? Music won’t make them disappear–and it shouldn’t. But it can shift the nervous system enough to create space for connection. Put on a slow or midtempo song and let the gentle rhythm help regulate your child while you stay present.

Mindful MOments for Kids

Mindful Moments for Kids—winner of Parents’ Choice Gold—is Kira’s unique collection of 30 one-minute mindfulness exercises for children. The simple, fun exercises can be done seated or standing, any time of day, making these perfect for classroom or home use, and great in the car!

Kira’s easy, kid-friendly techniques are designed to help children harness their energy, release anxiety, and develop self-awareness.
