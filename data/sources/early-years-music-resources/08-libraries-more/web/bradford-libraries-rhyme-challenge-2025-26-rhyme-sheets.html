Rhyme challenge rhyme sheets

Please note that we know that the rhyme sheets are not accessible. If you need help reading the information in them, please contact susan.brewster-craig@bradford.gov.uk.

See the sleeping bunnies

See the little bunnies sleeping till its nearly noon

Shall we wake them with a merry tune?

They're so still, are they ill?

No! Wake up bunnies!

Hope little bunnies, hop, hop, hop

Hope little bunnies, hop, hop, hop

Hope little bunnies, hop, hop, hop

Hope little bunnies, hop and stop

Row, row, row your boat

Row, row, row your boat, gently down the stream

Merrily, merrily, merrily, merrily, life is but a dream

Row, row, row your boat, gently down the stream

If you see a crocodile, don't forget to scream

Row, row, row your boat, gently down the river

If you see a polar bear, don't forget to shiver

Row, row, row your boat, gently to the shore

If you see a lion, don't forget to roar

Hello Library

(Sung to the tune of Baa, baa black sheep)

Hello Library, have you any books

Yes, I think so, let's go take a look

Go and tell your auntie

Go and tell a friend

Here at Bradford Libraries,

we've got lots of books to lend

Thank you said your auntie

And thank you said your friend

And thank you Bradford Libraries

with lots of books to lend
