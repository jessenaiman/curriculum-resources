## Notes
- **Public-domain status:** All 24 historical items are pre-1930 US publications (or UK works by authors deceased 70+ years: Gomme d. 1938, Crane d. 1915, Stanford d. 1924, Froebel d. 1852, Poulsson d. 1939 — UK/US status confirmed via the hosting archives' public-domain scans / Project Gutenberg USA clearance). Internet Archive items are unrestricted public downloads; Gutenberg items are "Public domain in the USA".
- **Format notes:** Scanned songbooks are image PDFs (music notation); full texts are Gutenberg transcriptions or Internet Archive OCR text captures. Newell (1903) is an OCR text capture because the IA PDF derivative was unavailable.
- **Multiple editions of the same lineage are intentionally included** to show change over time: Mother Goose's Melody (1843 US / 1889 facsimile of 1765 / 1843 Boston "Only True" / 1916 Real Mother Goose); Songs and Games for Little Ones (Jenks 1887 → Walker 1911); Songs of the Child-World (1897, 1904, 1915); graded school series (Progressive Music 1921 → Music Hour 1929).
