# My Garden

Here is my garden
I’ll rake it with care
And then some seeds we’ll plant in there
The sun will shine
And then my garden will grow big and tall


**Printed actions:**
Hand out and open, palm up
Rake with other hand
Drop seeds
Arms make sun
Stretch arms way up and out
