# The Falling Leaf, The Milkshake and The Cell Phone.

Offer instruments that children can play freely such as rhythm
sticks, bells, tambourines and bongo drums. Contact local
music stores for donations. Ask parents if they would like to donate to your music area.
• During your morning meeting, instead of discussing the weather, season or day of the
week you can clap, chant or sing it to a familiar tune.
• Recite a nursery rhyme every day before lunch.
• Chant a fun rhyme or song while cleaning.
• Do a meditation montra in preparation for nap or rest time
The use of words, rhymes and tempos assist children
in memory and recall.
