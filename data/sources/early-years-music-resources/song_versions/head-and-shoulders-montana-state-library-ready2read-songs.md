# Head and Shoulders

Head and shoulders, knees and toes,
knees and toes;
Head and shoulders, knees and toes,
knees and toes;
Eyes and ears and mouth and nose;
Head and shoulders, knees and toes,
Knees and toes! (touch as named; sing once, then sing again more
and more quickly until no one can keep up, or until you’re all
exhausted!)
