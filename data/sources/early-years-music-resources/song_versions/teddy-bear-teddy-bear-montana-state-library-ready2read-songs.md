# Teddy Bear, Teddy Bear

Teddy bear, teddy bear, turn around. (start in standing position
then turn around)
Teddy bear, teddy bear, touch the ground.
Teddy bear, teddy bear, tie your shoe.
Teddy bear, teddy bear, I love you!
Teddy bear, teddy bear, turn out the light. (pretend to turn out
light)
Teddy bear, teddy bear, say “Good night!”


**Printed actions:**
touch ground
pretend to tie shoe
give yourself a hug
blow kiss
