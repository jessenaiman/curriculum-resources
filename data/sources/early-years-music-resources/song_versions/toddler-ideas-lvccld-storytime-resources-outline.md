# Toddler Ideas:

• Over time, your group may become more familiar with your storytimes and you may add
another book or additional activities
• Incorporating sign language is a wonderful way to encourage vocabulary
• Make a song cube with well-known songs. The kids will love getting to toss the cube and sing
something familiar.
