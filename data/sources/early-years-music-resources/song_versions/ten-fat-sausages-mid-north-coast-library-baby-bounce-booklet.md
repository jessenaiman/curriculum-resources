# Ten Fat Sausages

Ten fat sausages sizzling in a pan
One went ‘pop’ and the other went ‘bang’
Now there’s eight fat sausages sizzling in a pan
Eight fat sausages…
Six fat sausages…
Four fat sausages…
Two fat sausages…
No more sausages sizzling in the pan!
