# Helpful Websites

instructional techniques and curricula are effective.

Educators Page: has links to information and resources of use to Early Childhood
educators and child care providers.
http://www.colorincolorado.org/—Colorín Colorado: an online service furnishing
information, activities, advice, and resources for educators and families of
Spanish-speaking English language learners.

Childhood education.

and links related to language and literacy.
