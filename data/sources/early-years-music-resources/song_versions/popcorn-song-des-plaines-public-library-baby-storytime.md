# POPCORN SONG

tune: “Frere Jacques”
Popcorn kernels, popcorn kernels
In the pot, in the pot
(make their scarves 'disappear' by bunching
them up in their fists)
Shake them shake them shake them,
shake them shake them shake them
'til they POP! ‘til they POP!


**Printed actions:**
wave scarves overhead
shake
toss scarves up into the air
