# PIZZA, PICKLE, PUMPERNICKEL

Pizza, pickle, pumpernickel,
My little baby needs a tickle!
Once on your nose,
Once on your toes,
And once on your tummy where the hot dog goes!
*Can replace hot dog with other foods*


**Printed actions:**
bounce
tickle baby
tap nose
tap toes
tickle tummy
