# Old McDonald

Old MacDonald had a farm, ee, ii, ee, ii, oo.
And on his farm he had a cow , ee, ii, ee, ii, oo.
With a “moo moo” here,
and a “moo moo” there
Here a “moo”
There a “moo”
Everywhere a :moo moo.”
Old MacDonald had a farm, ee, ii, ee, ii, ooooooooo.
Pig, “oink oink”; chick, “cluck cluck;” horse, “neigh neigh;” sheep, “baa baa;”
Turkey, “gobble gobble;” cats, “meow meow;” ducks, “quack quack”
