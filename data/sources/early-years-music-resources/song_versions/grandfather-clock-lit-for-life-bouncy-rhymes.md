# Grandfather Clock

The grandfather clock goes tick tock, tick tock,
tick tock, tick tock
The kitchen clock goes tick tock, tick tock, tick
tock, tick tock
But mommy’s little watch goes Tick-a, Tick-a,
tick-a, tick-a, tick-a (bounce faster or give a
tickle)


**Printed actions:**
rock side to side
a little faster
