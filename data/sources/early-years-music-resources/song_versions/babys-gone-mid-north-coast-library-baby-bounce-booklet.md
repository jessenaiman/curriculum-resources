# Baby’s Gone!

Baby’s gone, where is she?
Peek-a-boo! Now I see.
Gone again, where’d she go?
Peek-a-boo! I found her toe!
17


**Printed actions:**
Cover eyes with hands
Take hands away and ‘find’ baby
Cover eyes again
Tickle toes
