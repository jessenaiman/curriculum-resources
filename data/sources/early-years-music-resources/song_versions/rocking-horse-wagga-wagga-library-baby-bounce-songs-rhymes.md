# Rocking Horse

Rocking horse, rocking horse, to and fro,
*Gently rock backwards and forwards*
Side to side and away we go,
*Gently rock side to side*
Rocking horse, rocking horse, front and back,
*Gently rock backwards and forwards*
Don't fall off just like that. Woah!
*Gently & quickly lower baby*
Round and Round the Garden
Round and round the garden,
Like a teddy bear.
One step, two step,
Tickly under there!
