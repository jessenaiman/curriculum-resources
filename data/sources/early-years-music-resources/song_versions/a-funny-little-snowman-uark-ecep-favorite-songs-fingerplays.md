# A Funny Little Snowman

A funny little snowman,
had a carrot nose.
Along came a bunny
and what do you suppose?
That hungry little bunny
looking for his lunch,
Ate the snowman’s carrot with a nibble, nibble crunch!
Here is a Frog
Here is a frog sitting on a log
and here is a great big pond
when a bug she spies, she rolls her eyes
and splashes into the great big pond
X


**Printed actions:**
Make a fist with one hand
Hold other hand flat
Roll your eyes
Clap your fist into the other hand
