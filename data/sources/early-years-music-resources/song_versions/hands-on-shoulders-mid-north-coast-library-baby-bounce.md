# Hands On Shoulders

Hands on shoulders, hands on knees.
Hands behind you, if you please;
Touch your ears, then your nose,
Touch your belly and now your toes;
Hands up high in the air,
Down at your sides,
and touch your hair;
Hands up high as before,
Now clap your hands,
one-two-three-four!
