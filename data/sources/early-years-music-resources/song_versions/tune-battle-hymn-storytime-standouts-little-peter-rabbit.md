# Tune: Battle Hymn

Little Peter Rabbit had a fly upon his ear,
Use 2 fingers to make rabbit ears, thumb and pointer finger together, point to ear
Little Peter Rabbit had a fly upon his ear,
Repeat gestures
Little Peter Rabbit had a fly upon his ear,
Repeat gestures
And he flicked it 'til it flew away.
Flick at ear
Second time - do not sing "Rabbit," just make
gesture
Third time - do not sing "Rabbit" or "fly" just make gestures
Fourth time - do not sing "Rabbit," "fly" or "ear", just make
gestures
