# I Hear Thunder

I hear thunder, I hear thunder
So do I, so do I
Pitter-patter raindrops, pitter-patter raindrops
I’m all dry, so am I
I hear thunder, I hear thunder
Hark, don’t you? Hark, don’t you?
Pitter-patter raindrops, pitter-patter raindrops
I’m wet through, so are you!
