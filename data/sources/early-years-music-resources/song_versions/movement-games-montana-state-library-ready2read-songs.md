# Movement Games

Say the words loud or soft, fast or slow, for fun and variety.
Tall as a Tree
Tall as a tree,
Wide as a house,
Thin as a pin,
Small as a mouse.
Jack in the Box
Jack in the box,
Jack in the box,
Oh so still -
Won’t you come out?
Won’t you come out?
Won’t you come out?
YES I WILL!
Hickory Dickory Dock
Hickory dickory dock,
The mouse ran up the clock. (fi ngers on one hand run up arm, sit
atop head)
The clock struck one,
The mouse ran down -
Hickory dickory dock!
Additional verses:
Clock struck two,
the mouse said “Boo!”
Clock struck three,
the mouse said “Whee!”
Clock struck four,
the mouse said “No more!”


**Printed actions:**
stretch arms overhead
stretch arms out to sides
arms tight against sides
crouch
crouch down
shout and jump up
clasped hands swing like clock pendulum
clap hands one time
hand runs down again
swing pendulum again
clap twice
clap three times
clap four times
