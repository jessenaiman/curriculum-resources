# Three Cheeky Monkeys

Three cheeky monkeys jumping on the bed
One fell off and bumped his head
Mummy called the doctor and the doctor said
No more monkeys jumping on the bed!
Repeat for
Two cheeky monkeys…
One cheeky monkey...
