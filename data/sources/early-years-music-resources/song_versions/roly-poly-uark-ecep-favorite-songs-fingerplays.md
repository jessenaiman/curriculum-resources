# Roly Poly

Out, out, out!
Roly Poly,
Roly Poly,
Touch your toes.
Roly Poly,
Roly Poly,
Touch your nose!
Roly Poly,
Roly Poly,
To the sky.
Roly Poly,
Roly Poly,
Fly! Fly! Fly!


**Printed actions:**
Arms reach behind back
Arms twirl around in front of baby
Touch toes
Arms twirl around in front of baby
Touch nose
Arms twirl around in front of baby
Raise baby to standing position
Arms twirl around in front of baby
Raise baby into sky and fly from side to side
* Older children may do these actions on their own
