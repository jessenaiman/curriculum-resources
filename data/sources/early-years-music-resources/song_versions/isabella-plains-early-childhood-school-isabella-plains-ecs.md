# Isabella Plains Early Childhood School

learning and development through collaboration
50 Essential
Songs and Rhymes
for Preschool
The following booklet was developed by the 2015 Preschool team as an
additional strategy to help develop children’s vocabulary, their phonemic
awareness and to help develop a love of music and literature. The book is
intended to be used and built upon each year by the preschool team.
At the end of each year the book will be edited and updated for the following
year. We hope you enjoy singing and learning with the preschool children.
