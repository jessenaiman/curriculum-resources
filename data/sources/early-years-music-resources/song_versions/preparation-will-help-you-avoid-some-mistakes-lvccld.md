# Preparation Will Help You Avoid Some Mistakes

• Plan early
• Practice the material
• Create an outline
• Choose an opening and closing routine
• Know your space
• Know your audience
Key Components for a Great Storytime
• Set the mood or create an atmosphere at the beginning of each storytime.
• Welcome/Introductions
o Introduce yourself and welcome the families
o Introduce the storytime and age range
o Engage with children and parents
o Keep the welcome songs and routines the same each week
• Have an opening routine that feels comfortable to you. The ultimate goal is to have the kids
seated and ready for your first book.
• Choose songs to get the little ones moving!
• Choose books that are age appropriate and engaging
• Use transitions while moving from book to song to action rhyme
