# I’m Washing My Hands

I’m washing my hands, I’m washing my
hands, I’m washing my hands, I’m
washing my hands.
I wash each finger with soap and with
water.
Shake, Shake, Shake
Shake, shake, shake.
Shake, shake, shake.
Everybody, everybody, shake, shake,
shake.
Let’s go up, up, up.
Down, down, down.
Up, up, up
Down, down, down.
And…..shake, shake, shake.
Now go up, up STOP!
And down, down, down
And…..shake, shake, shake.
