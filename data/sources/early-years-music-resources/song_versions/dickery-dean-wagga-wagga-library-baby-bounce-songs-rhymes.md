# Dickery Dean

What is the matter with Dickery Dean?
He jumped right into the washing machine!
Chug, chug, chug, chug
Chug, chug, chug, chug
*Bounce side to side*
Nothing’s the matter with Dickery Dean
He dove in dirty, and he jumped out clean!
*Lift*
Five in the Bed
There were five in the bed
And the little one said, ‘Roll over, roll over’,
So they all rolled over and one fell out.
Repeat with four, three, two
There was one in the bed
And the little one said, ‘Goodnight!’.
Tummy time
practice!
