# Where is Thumbkin?

(Tune of Frere Jacques. The fingers on your hands “talk” to each other, one at a time. On “run and hide” put hands
behind your back. We only do two verses with toddlers.)
Where is Thumbkin, where is Thumbkin? Here I am! Here I am!
How are you today, sir? Very well I thank you.
Run away. Run away.
