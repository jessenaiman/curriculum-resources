# Five Little Monkeys

Five little monkeys
Jumping on the bed - (rest elbow on other hand, jump arm up and
down)
One fell off ,
And bumped his head!
Mama called the doctor
And the doctor said -
“No more monkeys
jumping on the bed!”
Mama called the doctor
And the doctor said -
“No more monkeys jumping on the bed!”
