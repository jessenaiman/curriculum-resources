# Little Mouse

Hurry scurry little mouse,
Starts at your toes.
Hurry scurry little mouse,
Past your knees he goes.
Hurry scurry little mouse,
Past where your tummy is.
Hurry scurry little mouse,
Gives you a mousy kiss.
