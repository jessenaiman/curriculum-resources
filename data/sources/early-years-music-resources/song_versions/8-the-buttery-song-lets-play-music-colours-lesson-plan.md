Butterﬂy, butterﬂy, butterﬂy blue,
I’d like to ﬂy like you!
*
Butterﬂy, butterﬂy, butterﬂy pink,
What do you think, butterﬂy pink?
Butterﬂy, butterﬂy, butterﬂy pink,
What do you think, butterﬂy pink?
*
Butterﬂy, butterﬂy, butterﬂy yellow,
Let me catch you, just to say Hello!
Butterﬂy, butterﬂy, butterﬂy yellow,
Let me catch you, just to say Hello!

Butterﬂy, butterﬂy, butterﬂy green,

Where have you been butterﬂy green?
9. The Butterﬂy Song is very easy to pick up largely due to its
repetitive phrases see the chime bar clip on the blog . If you
combine this song with the accompanying craft activity the
children can make their own butterﬂies to ﬂutter as they sing
the song. These little butterﬂies are very simple and quick to
make and are a lovely way to bring The Butterﬂy Song to life.
