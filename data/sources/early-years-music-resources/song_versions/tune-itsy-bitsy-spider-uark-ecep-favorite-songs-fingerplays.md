# (Tune: Itsy Bitsy Spider)

elephants trunk)
Went out one day to play
Down came the rain (Finger flutter, representing rain falling
to the ground)
and then he couldn’t stay
But up came the sun (Arms above head forming a circle for the
sun)
And dried up all the rain
And the great big elephant
Went out to play again.

I’m fine!
And I hope that you are too!
