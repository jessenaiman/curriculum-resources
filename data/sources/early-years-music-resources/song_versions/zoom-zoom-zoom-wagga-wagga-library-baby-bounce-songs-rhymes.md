# Zoom Zoom Zoom

Zoom, zoom, zoom,
We're going to the moon.
Zoom, zoom, zoom,
We'll get there very soon.
*Gently sway baby in air on on knees*
5, 4, 3, 2, 1,
*Bounce to the countdown*
Blast off!
*lift*
