to the side tootsie tootsie, tootsie roll
other side tootsie tootsie, tootsie roll

Tap our sticks today
Let’s all tap our sticks today, sticks today, sticks today.

Tap your sticks in the air

This is the way
this is the way we tap our sticks, tap our sticks, tap our sticks.
