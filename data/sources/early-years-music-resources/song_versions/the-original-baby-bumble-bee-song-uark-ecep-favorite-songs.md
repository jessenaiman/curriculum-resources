# The “Original” Baby Bumble Bee Song

I’m bringing home a baby bumble bee.
Won’t my mommy be so proud of me!
I’m bringing home a baby bumble bee
OUCH! He stung me!
I’m squishing up the baby bumble bee.
Won’t my mommy be so proud of me!
I’m squishing up the baby bumble bee
Ooooo! He’s all over me!
I’m wiping off the baby bumble bee.
Won’t my mommy be so proud of me!
I’m wiping off the baby bumble bee
LOOK! He’s all gone!
Manners
Cover your mouth when you cough,
Cover your mouth when you sneeze
If you cough or if you sneeze --
Cover your mouth if you please!


**Printed actions:**
Pretend to cup a bee inside your hands, swinging it
Press and rub your hands together
Wipe your hands off on the sides of your legs
Tune: Hickory, Dickory, Dock
Make a sneeze into your elbow
