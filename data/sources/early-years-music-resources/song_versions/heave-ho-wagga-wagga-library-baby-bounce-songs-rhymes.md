Here is the Beehive
Here is the beehive, where are the bees?
*Hold your fist up in the air*
Hidden away where nobody sees.
*Move the other hand around your fist*
Watch and you'll see them come out of the hive.
*Bend your head close to the fist, and "look" inside it!*
One...two...three...four...five!
*Count on your fingers*
Buzz...buzz...buzz...buzz...buzz.

*Lift legs up*
Here we come down, down, down,
*Legs back down*
Here we go backwards and forwards
*Rock backwards and forwards*
And here we go round and around!
*Bicycle legs around*

Hickory Dickory Dock
Hickory Dickory Dock
*Gently bounce your baby to the beat*
The mouse ran up the clock
*Run your fingers up from baby's toes
to their chin and tickle*
The clock struck one
*Clap once*
The mouse ran down
Hickory Dickory Dock
*Run your fingers back down to
baby's toes and tickle*
Repeat for
The clock struck two
The mouse went "boo!"
*Clap twice. Hide baby's eyes with your hands
then pull them away on the word "boo!"*
The clock struck three
The mouse went "weeee!"
*Clap three times. Lift your baby up
into the air on the word "weeee!"*
The clock struck four
The mouse said "NO MORE!"
*Clap four times. Shake your
finger on the words "no more!"*
