# A Sailor Went To Sea

A Sailor went to sea*,
sea, sea.
To see what he could see,
see, see.
But all that he could see,
see, see.
Was the bottom of the sea, sea, sea.
Repeat three times, each time a little bit
faster. On the fourth time, omit the words
"sea" and "see", just salute.
*Each time you sing "sea or see," bring your hand to
your forehead in a salute.
