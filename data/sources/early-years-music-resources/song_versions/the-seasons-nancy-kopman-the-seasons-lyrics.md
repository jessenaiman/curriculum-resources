# The Seasons

Spring begins March 21, mud and sunshine, Winter’s done
April, May and June bring grass and flowers....
July comes by with summer skies, August bees and butterflies....
September’s colours burn away the hours....
Autumn rides October’s wind, November: Winter’s creeping in....
December’s cold for nature’s sleepy reasons.
January’s icy moon, February’s cold, but soon, we March back in and again
begin the Seasons!
