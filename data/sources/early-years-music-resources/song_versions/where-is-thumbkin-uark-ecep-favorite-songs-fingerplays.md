# Where is Thumbkin

Where is thumb-kin? Where is Thumb-kin?
Here I am, here I am.
How are you this morning
Ver-y well, I thank you.
Run and play, run and play.
1. Where is Pointer
2. Where is Tall One
3. Where is Ring Finger
4. Where is Pinky
5. Where is The Whole Family


**Printed actions:**
Tune: “Are You Sleeping”
Place hands behind back
Show one thumb, then other
Bend one thumb
Bend other thumb
Wiggle thumbs away
2nd finger
middle finger
fourth finger
fifth finger
whole hand
