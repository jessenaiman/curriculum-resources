# I Caught a Fish Alive Rub-a-Dub-Dub

Row, row, row
your boat,
Gently down
the stream.
Merrily, merrily,
merrily, merrily,
Life is
but a dream.
1, 2, 3, 4, 5,
I caught a fish alive!
6, 7, 8, 9, 10,
I let him go again!
Rub-a-dub-dub,
three men in a tub,
And who do you
think they be?
The butcher,
the baker,
the candlestick maker.
And all went
out to sea!
