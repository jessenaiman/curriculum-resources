# Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes

Head and shoulders knees and toes,
knees and toes, knees and toes,
Head and shoulders knees and toes,
we all clap hands together!
Eyes and ears and mouth and nose,
mouth and nose, mouth and nose
Eyes and ears and mouth and nose,
we all clap hands together!
