# Movements for Baby

Down by the meadow
in an itty bitty pool
Swam three little fishes
and a mommy fishy, too
Swim,” said the mommy fishy
“Swim, if you can”
So they swam and they swam
all over the dam
Boop, boop, ditta, datta (Cross left leg over right,
Wattem, shoo! then right leg over left)
Boop, boop, ditta, datta (Cross left leg over right,
wattem, shoo! then right leg over left)
Boop, boop, ditta, datta (Cross left leg over right,
wattem, shoo! then right leg over left)
And they swam and they swam
all over the dam!
