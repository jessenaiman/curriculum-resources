# Gingerbread Man

Mix and stir and pat in the pan Chopping motion with
baby’s hand
I’m going to make a gingerbread man
With a nose so neat, Poke nose
And a smile so sweet, Smile
Gingerbread shoes on your gingerbread feet
Move baby’s feet
