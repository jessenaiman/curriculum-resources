# Pony Boy

Pony boy, pony boy
Won’t you be my pony boy?
Here we go,
Don’t go slow,
Giddy-up, giddy-up, giddy-up
Whoa!

Chicken in the Barnyard

Staying out of trouble.

**Printed actions:**
Repeat with pony girl
Repeat with more barn yard animals
To the tune of “This is the Way We Wash Our Clothes”
Bounce baby gently on lap
Bounce baby a little more vigorously on lap
Bounce baby fast on lap
Draw a little circle in child’s palm
Slowly creep fingers up child’s arm
Tickle child’s underarm, or move in for a gobble on her neck
