# The Sea

Tune: Wheels on the Bus
from Storytime Katie
ch/
The waves on the sea go up and down, up
and down, up and down
The waves on the sea go up and down all day
long.
Repeat with:
The shark in the sea goes snap, snap, snap…
The fish in the sea goes swish, swish, swish…
The boats on the sea go toot, toot, toot…
