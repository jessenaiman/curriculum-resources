# I'm a Little Teapot

I'm a little teapot, short and stout
Here is my handle
Here is my spout
When I get all steamed up, hear me shout
Tip me over and pour me out!


**Printed actions:**
one hand on hip
other arm out straight
As song ends, lean over and tip arm out like a spout.
