# Looking Through My Window

(Cut a pretend "window" through a box,
or use a lightweight empty picture frame.
Peek various toys and stuffies through the opening!)
Lookin' through my window, what do I see?
I see , looking at me!
Repeat with: Puppy Dog, Birdie, Bunny, Squirrel,
fluffy Cloud and so forth--anything
you see while looking out
through your windows!
