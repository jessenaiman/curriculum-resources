# Preschool is Over

Preschool is over, it’s time to say goodbye.
We take our little hands and we wave bye-bye.
Optional… it can be nice to do a special goodbye ritual to each child. Some teachers use a rubber stamp to give every
child a stamp on their hand. Some have a puppet that goes around and gives everyone a kiss goodbye.
