# Down By the Bay

Down by the bay
Where the watermelons grow
Back to my home
I dare not go
For if I do
My mother will say
Did you ever see a fly
Wearing a tie?
Down by the bay.

Where the watermelons grow
Back to my home
I dare not go
For if I do
My mother will say
Did you ever see a whale
With a polka dot tail?
Did you ever see a moose
Kissing a goose?
