# Free Imaginative Musical Movement

Using brightly coloured, scarves, ribbons or streamers. Explain
that there will be 4 different short music tracks and see if they
can guess which mood each one represents. Once you have
played and identiﬁed the tracks, encourage the kids to move
freely with the scarves or ribbons to help them express the
different moods.
Happy - Handel’s Arrival of The Queen Of Sheba
