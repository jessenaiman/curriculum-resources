# Fire Truck

Hurry, hurry,
Drive the fi re truck.
Hurry, hurry,
Drive the fi re truck.
Hurry, hurry,
Drive the fi re truck.
Ding, ding, ding, ding, ding!
Hurry, hurry,
Turn the corner.
Hurry, hurry,
Turn the corner.
Hurry, hurry,
Turn the corner.
Ding, ding, ding, ding, ding!
Repeat with:
Climb the ladder.
Spray the water.


**Printed actions:**
put hands up like they are on the steering wheel
pretend to ring bell
hands on steering wheel but lean into the corner
pretend to ring bell
climb the ladder
spray the water
