# Tick Tock Clock

Tick tock, tick tock. (rock child from side to
side)
I'm a little cuckoo clock.
Tick tock, tick tock, Stop!
What time is it? It's one o'clock!
Cuckoo!


**Printed actions:**
stop rocking
bounce child up once – or lift in air
Repeat with 2 and 3 o’clock
