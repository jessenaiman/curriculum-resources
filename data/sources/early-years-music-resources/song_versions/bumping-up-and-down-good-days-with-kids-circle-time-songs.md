# Bumping Up and Down

Bumping up and down in my little red wagon  Won’t you be my darlin’?
One wheel’s off and the axle’s broken …
’s gonna fix it with a hammer …


**Printed actions:**
Tune of 10 Little Indians. Bounce babies on knees. When wheel’s off, bounce to the side. Hammer with their hands.
