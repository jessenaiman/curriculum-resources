# What is Baby Bounce?

Baby Bounce is about using rhymes, songs and books
to introduce the sounds and rhythm of language. It
helps babies to understand that words and pictures
tell stories and have meaning, and increases
vocabulary.
It is also about making connections in the community
during the early years of parenthood.
