# Chapter Five

Teaching Music Concepts in Kindergarten. This chapter presents teaching strategies for core
readiness concepts in the Early Childhood classroom based on the model of learning pre -
sented in Chapter Four. Teaching strategies include how to teach the concepts of loud and
soft, beat, high and low related chants and melodies, fast and slow, and rhythm.
