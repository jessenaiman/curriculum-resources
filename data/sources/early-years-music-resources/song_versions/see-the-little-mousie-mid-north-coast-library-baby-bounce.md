# See The Little Mousie

See the little mousie
Creeping up the stairs
Looking for a warm nest
There oh there!


**Printed actions:**
make a circular motion on baby’s hand or tummy
walk up arm with two fingers
walk fingers around shoulder
tickle gently under arm
