# Come A’Look A’See

Come a’look a’see, here’s my mama
Come a’look a’see, here’s my papa
Come a’look a’see, my brother tall
Sister, baby, I love them all!


**Printed actions:**
Hold up each finger as you sing and then clasp hands together
