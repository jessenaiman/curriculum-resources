# One Bright Scarf

One bright scarf waiting for the wind to blow
*Gentle wave scarf in the air*
Wiggle it high and wiggle it low
*Wiggle scarf high then low*
Shake it fast and shake it slow
*Wiggle scarf fast then slow*
Where did it go?
*Hide the scarf behind your back*
Here it is!
*Bring the scarf back*
