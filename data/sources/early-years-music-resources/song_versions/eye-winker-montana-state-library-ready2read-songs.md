# Eye Winker

Eye winker,
Tom Tinker,
Nose smeller,
Mouth eater,
Chin chopper,
Chin chopper,
Chin chopper,
Chin chopper, chin.


**Printed actions:**
point to eyes
point to ears
point to nose
point to mouth
tap chin
gently tickle under chin
