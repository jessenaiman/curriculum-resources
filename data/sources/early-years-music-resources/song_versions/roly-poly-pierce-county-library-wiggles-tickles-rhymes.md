# Roly Poly

Roly poly, roly poly
Up, up, up…up, up, up
Roly poly poly, roly poly poly,
Down, down, down…down, down, down
(Roll your arms in a circle, lift arms up
over head and then bring them back down)
(This is also a fun song to sing while
throwing and catching a ball)
30


**Printed actions:**
To the tune of “Frère Jacques”
