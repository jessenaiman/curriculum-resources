# Bumpy Road

Smooth road, smooth road. Move baby in circle
Bumpy road, bumpy road. Bounce baby
Rough road, rough road. Increase height of bounce
Hole. Gently drop baby between legs
Jelly in the Bowl
Jelly in the bowl, jelly in the bowl Wiggle baby
Wibble, wobble, wibble, wobble, Wiggle side to side
Jelly in the bowl. Wiggle
Cookies in the jar, cookies in the jar, Bounce baby
Shake them up, shake them up, Bounce side to side
Cookies in the jar. Bounce
Candles on the cake, candles on the cake, Lean in circle
Blow them out, blow them out, Rock back and forth
Pfft, pfft, pfft! Blow on hair and lie them down
