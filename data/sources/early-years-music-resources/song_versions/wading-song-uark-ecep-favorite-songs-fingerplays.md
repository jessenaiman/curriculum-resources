# Wading Song

She waded in the water and she got her feet all wet,
She waded in the water and she got her feet all wet,
She waded in the water and she got her feet all wet
But she didn’t get her  wet, yet!
She waded in the water and she got her knees wet,
She waded in the water and she got her knees wet,
She waded in the water and she got her knees wet,
But she didn’t get her  wet, yet!
She waded in the water and she got her thighs wet,
She waded in the water and she got her thighs wet,
She waded in the water and she got her thighs wet,
But she didn’t get her  wet, yet!
She waded in the water but she didn’t get it wet,
She waded in the water but she didn’t get it wet,
She waded in the water but she didn’t get it wet,
No, she didn’t get her bathing suit wet, yet!

The Wheels on the Bus
The wheels on the bus go round and round,
round and round, round and round.
The wheels on the bus go round and round
all through the town.
The horn on the bus says “beep, beep, beep
The wipers on the bus go swish, swish, swish
The driver on the bus says :”move on back!”
The lights on the bus go blink, blink, blink
The baby on the bus cries “waa, waa, waa”
The mommy on the bus says “sh, sh, sh”
The kids on the bus go bump, bump, bump
