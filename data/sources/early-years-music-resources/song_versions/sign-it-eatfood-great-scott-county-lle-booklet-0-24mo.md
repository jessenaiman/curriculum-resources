# Sign It: eat/food

Read a book more than once to increase your child’s vocabulary. Bookshave three times  as many rare words than conversation.Starting around 9 months, babies may open books and begin to turnpages. Board book pages are easier for babies to grasp. Remember,it’s OK to skip pages!
For babies, hide objects under a blanket or a cup. Have them find them.
As your child begins to crawl and walk, play hide and seek, taking turns
to find each other.
