# Finger Family

Finger family’s up
And finger family’s down
Finger family’s dancing
All around the town
Dance them on your shoulders
Dance them on your head
Dance them on your knees and
Tuck them into bed
(You can also do this rhyme with
individual fingers, Peter Pointer, etc.)
