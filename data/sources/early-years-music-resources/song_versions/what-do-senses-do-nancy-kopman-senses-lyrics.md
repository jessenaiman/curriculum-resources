# What do Senses Do

What do senses do?
They can see, they can hear and they
can
taste, smell, touch.
What do senses do?
The five different senses can tell
you so much.
Can you see grass or trees?
Can you see a forest, cars or people
talking?
Can you see birds or bees?
Can you see any animals out walking?
Sounds can be loud like a train.
Thunder is loud, and there are quiet
sounds, too:
Like a whisper, footsteps or rain.
What kinds of sounds are special just to
you?
What kinds of things taste sour?
Do you like salty, or do you like sweet?
There are some things that taste bitter,
now are you thinking about the things
you like to eat?
Is somebody baking bread?
Is somebody cooking, or is something
burning?
What’s happening in your head?
Breathe in through your nose and tell
me
what you’re learning.
Can you feel sharp or dull?
Can you feel something that is wet or
dry?
Can you feel warm or cold?
What do things feel like when you close
your eyes?
Faces
What do faces tell you?
Faces don’t let feelings hide.
Each part of your face can
show what you are feeling inside.
What do faces show you?
You see faces all the time.
Each part of a face shows
feelings just like yours and like mine.
Just look at your eyebrows.
They move up and they can move down.
Whatever you are feeling,
you’re showing everybody around
how you feel inside.
Just look at your nose.
The sides of it move in and move out.
Whatever you are feeling
your nose can show a little about
what you feel inside.
Lips move all around
to help make sounds like “ahh” and
“ooh”.
Whenever you are speaking
you’re sharing what’s inside of you.
