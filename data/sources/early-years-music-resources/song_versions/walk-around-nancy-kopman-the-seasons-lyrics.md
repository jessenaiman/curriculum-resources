# Walk Around

Walk around and walk around and walk around....walk around with me (re-
peat)
Now bounce, bounce, bounce....bounce, bounce, bounce....bounce, bounce,
bounce with me
Now side to side....side to side....side to side with me
Now turn around....turn around....turn around with me
Walk around and walk around and walk around....and now you can sit down
with me.
