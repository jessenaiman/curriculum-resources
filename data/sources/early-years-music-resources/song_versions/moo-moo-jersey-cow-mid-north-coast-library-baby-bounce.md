# Moo, Moo Jersey Cow

Moo, moo jersey cow, have you any milk
Yes sir, yes sir, three buckets full
One for the dog – woof woof
One for the cat – meow
And one for the little boy who likes to drink that
Thank you said the dog – woof woof
Thank you said the cat – meow
Thank you said the little boy, who likes to drink that.
