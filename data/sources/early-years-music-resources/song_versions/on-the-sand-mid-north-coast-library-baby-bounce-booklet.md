# On The Sand

Digging, digging with my spade
See the great big hole I’ve made
Now I’ll build a castle high
Right up to the bright blue sky
I will make my castle grand
With shells I’ve found upon the sand
Then I’ll gather seaweed too
And I’ll bring some home for you
70
Inanay
