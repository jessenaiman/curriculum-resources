# Rocking, Rocking…

Toast in the Toaster
Toast in the toaster, Bounce baby
Getting very hot,
Tick Tock Tick Tock, Rock baby side to side
Up you pop! Lift baby off lap and give little swing
Chop, Chop, Choppity Chop
Chop, Chop, Choppity Chop Chopping motion with
baby’s hand
Cut off the bottoms Chop hand along feet
And cut of the top Chop hand along forehead
And what there is left Tickle tummy
You put it in the pot
Chop, Chop, Choppity Chop
