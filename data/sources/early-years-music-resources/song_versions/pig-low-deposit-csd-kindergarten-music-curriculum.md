# Pig: Low

_What kind of voice does Baby Bear,
Mama Bear and Papa Bear Have in
the story of “The Three Bears?”
-
_What does upward movement
sound like?
-What does downward movement
sound like?
-What does it sound like when the
note does not move at all? What is a
repeating note/pitch?
-What does it sound like when the
music hops and jumps all around on
different pitches?
Students will be able to:
-Perceive and respond to the difference
between high, medium and low tones within
a song.
-Differentiate between high, medium, and
low sounds and find a pattern with high and
low tones.
-Catagorize animals sounds in terms of high,
low and medium pitch.
-Enact the Story of “The Three Bears: using
high, medium and low voices/pitch.
-Identify upward and downward movement
in music.
-Follow the upward or downward direction
of a melody using hand signals.
-Respond to the upward and downward
movement of a melody through movement
and dance.
-Identify same pitch. Repeated pitch.
-Identify pitches that jump and hop around.
-Respond to leaps and jumps in tone through
movement and dance..
-Create sound compositions using high,
low and middle pitched sounds.
-Enact stories and poems assigning high,
medium and low voices to each character.
-Movement/dance exercises enacting
high/Med/low tones.
-Movement/Dance exercises enacting
upward and downward tonal movement.
-Creative Dance
-Listening Activities
-Activities: (1) Draw What You Hear.
(2) Create Dances and movements to
accompany listening examples.(3) Puppet
