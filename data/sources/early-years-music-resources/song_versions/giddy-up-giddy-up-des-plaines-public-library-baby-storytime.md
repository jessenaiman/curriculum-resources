# GIDDY-UP, GIDDY-UP

Giddy-up, giddy-up ride to town
Giddy-up, giddy-up up and down
Giddy-up fast
Giddy up slow
Giddy-up, giddy-up, giddy-up, whoa!


**Printed actions:**
bounce babies on knees
lift baby up and down
bounce quickly
bounce slowly
dip baby backwards gently
