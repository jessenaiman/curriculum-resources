# The Grand Old Duke of York

The Grand Old Duke of York,
He had ten thousand men.
He marched them up to the top of the hill
Lift baby up in air
And he marched them down again!
Bring baby down to floor
And when they're up, they're up. Lift baby up
And when they're down, they're down.
Bring baby down
And when they're only half-way up,
Hold baby waist high
They're neither up nor down!
Lift baby up and then bring down again
Oh he marched them to the left. Lean to left
And he marched them to the right. Lean to right
Then he marched them round and round Move in circle
Oh what a silly sight!
