# Dino Stomp

The dinosaurs were dancing round the prehistoric swamp
They shook their heads, swished their tails
And did the dino stomp, stomp, stomp, stomp.
Dance dino, dance dino
Stomp, stomp, stomp
Dance dino, dance dino
Stomp, stomp, stomp!
They shook their head, swished their tails and did the dino stomp.
The dinosaurs were walking through the prehistoric mist
They shook their heads, swished their tails
And did the dino twist, twist, twist, twist.
Twist dino, twist dino,
twist, twist, twist.
Twist dino, twist dino
Twist, twist, twist.
They shook their head, swished their tails
And did the dino twist.
Everybody do this
Everybody do this, do this, do this
Everybody do this, just like me.
