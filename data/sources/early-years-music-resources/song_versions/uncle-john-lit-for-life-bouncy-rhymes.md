# Uncle John

Father and mother and Uncle John
Went to market one by one
Father fell off
Mother fell off
But Uncle John went on and on and on and on.
Up to the Ceiling
Daddy lifts me up to the ceiling,
Daddy swings me down to the floor.
Daddy, Daddy! More! More! More!
Up to the ceiling, down to the floor.
