# I’m An Airplane (Tune: Frere Jacques)

I’m an airplane  Bounce baby on knees
Flying high
Watch me tilt this way  Lean to the left
In the sky,
Additional Verses:
-Watch me tilt that way Lean to right
-Watch me do a landing Slide child down knees
On the ground
Baby, Baby, Smile At Me
Baby, baby, smile at me, Bounce child on knees
Smile at me, smile at me,
Baby, baby, smile at me,
I love you, my baby. Kiss child’s cheek
Additional Verses:
-Baby, baby, clap with me, Clap hands
-Baby, baby, dance with me, Gently sway child
