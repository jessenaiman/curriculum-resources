# I Blew One Big Bubble

I blew one big bubble,
I blew one big bubble,
I blew one big bubble,
I hope that it won’t pop…pop…pop
I hope that it won’t pop!
(Repeat with two big bubbles,
clapping twice after each “pop”)
7


**Printed actions:**
clap once after your sing each “pop”
Then three big bubbles, etc.
