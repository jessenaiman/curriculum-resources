“Miss Spider ”
Verses:
Vi-no el vien-to,
la hi-zo bai-lar,
vi-no la tor-men-ta,
la hi-zo ba-jar.
Literal translation:
Miss spider went for a stroll,
She spun a thread and tried to climb up,
Along came a wind that caused her to dance,
Along came a rain storm that made her come down.
