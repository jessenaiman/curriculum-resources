# Rickety Roo

Rickety roo, Rickety ree,
Bouncing on your Daddy’s knee.
Rickety roo, rickety row,
Stop the horsy,
Whooaaa.
Rickety roo, ruckety rup,
Going up, up, up.
Rickety roo, ricketry round,
Watch  going down!
Ride the Horse to Boston
Ride the horse to Boston
Ride the horse to Lynn
Watch out baby or you might fall in!
Robin on a Pole
There was a little robin
Sitting on a pole
Weeble wobble, weeble wobble,
Plop down the hole!
Robins
A robin and a robin’s son
Went to town to buy a bun
They couldn’t decide on plum or plain
And so they went back home again
She Fell into the Bathtub
She fell into the bathtub
She fell into the sink
She fell into the raspberry jam (fall between
legs)
And came out pink
We put her in the backyard and left her in the
rain
By half past suppertime
It washed her clean again
