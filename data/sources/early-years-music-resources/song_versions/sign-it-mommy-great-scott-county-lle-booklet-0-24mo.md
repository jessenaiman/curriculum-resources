# Sign It: mommy

Talk to your child! Keep a running conversation during diaper changes,bath times or as you prepare meals. They will learn about speakingincluding inflections and intonations. As babies grow older, they willbegin responding. Offer a toy and describe it. Is a ball bumpy or smooth? What colors areit?
Talking is a whole body experience. As your baby begins to move,
respond to their movements by talking to them.
Sing songs and say rhymes with movements to them.
