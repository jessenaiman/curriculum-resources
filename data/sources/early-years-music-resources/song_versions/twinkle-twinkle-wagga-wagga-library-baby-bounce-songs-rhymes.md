# Twinkle Twinkle

Twinkle twinkle little star
*wriggle fingers*
How I wonder what you are
*place finger on chin*
Up above the world so high
*point to the sky*
Like a diamond in the sky
*shape fingers into a diamond*
Twinkle twinkle little star
*wriggle fingers*
How I wonder what you are
*place finger on chin*
