# Five & Under Ideas:

• Ideas for this group can be pulled from Babytime, Toddler, and Preschool
• Over time, you will get a feel for the children and caregivers who attend, and what transitional
activities work best for them
• Be prepared to be flexible, have a variety of books and activities that you are familiar with that
can be substituted based on the input from your group
