# A Chubby Little

Snowman
A chubby little snowman
Had a carrot nose
Along came a bunny
And what do you suppose?
That hungry little bunny
Who was looking for some lunch
Ate the snowman’s carrot nose
Nibble, nibble, crunch!


**Printed actions:**
Chubby Snowman- Form a circle with your arms in front of your
body to mimic the snowman’s round tummy.
Carrot Nose- Grab at your nose, pinch fingers together and pull
hand away as if your nose was a long carrot.
Bunny- Hold hand up pointing middle and pointer fingers up
like bunny ears and bounce arm in front of you to mimic a
hopping bunny.
What Do You Suppose?- Hold hands out to side as if asking a
question.
