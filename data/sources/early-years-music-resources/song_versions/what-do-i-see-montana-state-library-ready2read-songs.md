# What Do I See?

What do I see? Baby’s knee. (with one fi nger, draw a circle on baby’s
knee; at the end, gently tickle baby’s knee)
Tickly, tickly, tic, tac, tee.
One for a penny, two for a pound,
Tickly, tickly, ‘round and ‘round.
