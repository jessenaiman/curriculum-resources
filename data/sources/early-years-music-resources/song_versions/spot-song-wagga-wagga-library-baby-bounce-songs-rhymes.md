# Spot Song

Put a spot over here and a spot over there.
Put a spot on your ear and a spot on your hair.
Put a lot of little spots in the air, everywhere!
It’s a spotty kind of day.
Repeat for
Put a stripe over here and a stripe over there…
Put a swirl over here and a swirl over there…
Sing along to this at
bath time with a
facewasher! Or anytime
using a silk scarf.
