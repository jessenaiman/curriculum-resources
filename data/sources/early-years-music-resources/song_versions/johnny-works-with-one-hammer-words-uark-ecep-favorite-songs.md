# Johnny Works with One Hammer Words

Johnny works with one hammer,
one hammer, one hammer
Johnny works with one hammer
Then he works with two


**Printed actions:**
1 hammer - 1 fist pounding on knee
2 hammers - 2 fists pounding on knee
3 hammers - 2 fists 1 foot
4 hammers - 2 fists 2 feet
5 hammers - 2 fists, 2 feet and head
after 5: “Then he goes to sleep.”
