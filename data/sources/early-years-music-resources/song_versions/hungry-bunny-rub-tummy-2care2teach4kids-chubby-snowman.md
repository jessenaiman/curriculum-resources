# Hungry Bunny- Rub tummy.

Looking For Lunch- Hold hand straight out over top of eyes and
look around.
Nibble, Nibble, Crunch- Open and close thumb and fingers like
your fingers are nibbling something, then for “crunch clap”
hands together.
