# Topic: Rhythm Grade K Time:

Essential Questions: Why is music so much fun? What makes music?
Performance Indicators Guided Questions Essential Knowledge & Skills Classroom Ideas Assessment Ideas
Standards 1,2,3 & 4
-What is sound?
-What is a beat?
-What is a steady beat?
- What is long and short
sound?
-What is a strong beat?
-What does music that
sounds like walking,
marching, hopping,
running or skipping look
like?
-What does Stop and Go
mean?
-What does silence look
and sound look like in
music?
-Why do musicians need
to have rests in music?
-What is a rhythmic
pattern?
-What is fast, medium and
slow tempo?
-What does 2, 3, and 4
4 4 4
meter feel like?
Students will be able to:
-Feel vibration and know that
Vibration = sound.
-Identify and move to a steady
beat.
-Keep a steady beat while listening, singing
& playing a rhythm instrument.
-Identify long and short sounds
-Demonstrate a strong beat
through movement and performance.
- Match locomotor movements
to the feel of a section of
music.
-Identify rhythmic
notation for specific movements.
