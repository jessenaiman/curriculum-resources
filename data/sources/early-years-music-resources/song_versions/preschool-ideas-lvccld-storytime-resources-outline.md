# Preschool Ideas:

• Using flannel to retell a story offer a different visual component. Counting rhymes are great as
flannels because you can make them as interactive as you want depending on your group size
• Having graphics or lyrics posters can help encourage audience participation
• Using manipulatives like scarves and shakers is a great way to make your music interactive
• Simple crafts and takeaways such as worksheets are a fun way to extend the storytime
