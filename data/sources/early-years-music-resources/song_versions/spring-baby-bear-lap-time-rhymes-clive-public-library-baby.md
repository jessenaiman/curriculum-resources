# sPRING Baby Bear Lap Time Rhymes

Week 3
Take your little hands and go
clap, clap, clap
Take your little toes and go
Tap, tap, tap
Take your little arms and go
Flap, flap, flap
Hooray!
Week 2
To market, to market, to buy a fat pig,
Home again, home again, jiggety-jig
To market to market, to buy a fat hog,
Home again, home again, jiggety-jog,
To market, to market to buy a plum bun,
Home again, home again, market is done.
Week 4
Snuggle up together
And clap, clap, clap
Snuggle up together
And tap, tap, tap
We’re working out together
Baby don’t stop
We’re working out together
Now hop, hop, hop!
Week 1
Here we go up, up, up
Here we go down, down, down (put baby back into
lap)
Here we go backwards
Here we go forwards
Here we go round and round (Hold baby close and
move your upper body in a circular motion)
Week 6
Ride baby ride, ch ch ch ch ch
Ride that horsey ride
Ride baby ride, ch ch ch ch ch
Ride that horsey ride
Whooooaaaa!
Week 8
Cheek chin cheek chin cheek chin, nose
Cheek chin cheek chin cheek chin, toes
Cheek chin cheek chin, up baby goes!


**Printed actions:**
Follow actions
Bounce baby on knees and sing rhyme
snuggle baby
clap baby’s hands
snuggle baby
tap baby’s feet
bounce baby
hop baby up and down 3xs
lift baby
lean far back
lean forward
bunce baby on lap
do again but whisper
lean back with baby
touch baby’s nose
touch baby’s toes
lift baby
