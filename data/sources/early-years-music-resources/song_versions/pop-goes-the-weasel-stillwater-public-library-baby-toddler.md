# Pop! Goes the Weasel

All around the cobbler's bench,
the monkey chased the weasel.
The monkey thought 'twas all in fun--
POP! goes the weasel.
A penny for a spool of thread, a penny for a needle.
That's the way the money goes--
POP! goes the weasel.


**Printed actions:**
Bounce or clap.
Lift Baby in the air!
Lift Baby in the air!
