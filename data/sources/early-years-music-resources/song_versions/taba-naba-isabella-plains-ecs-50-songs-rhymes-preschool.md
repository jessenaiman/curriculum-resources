# Taba Naba

Taba naba naba no rem
Tugi penai siri
Dinghy e naba we
Miko keimi
Sere re naba we
Taba naba nor em
Translation:
Come on let's go to the reef
Get into the dinghy when the morning tide is low
Let us row to the edge of the reef
Come on let's go to the reef.


**Printed actions:**
repeated 3 times
