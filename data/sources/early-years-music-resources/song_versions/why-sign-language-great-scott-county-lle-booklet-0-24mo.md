# Why Sign Language?

When you and your child
are able to communicate,
you create a closer bond.
Have a baby look at both of you in a mirror. Point out baby’s noseand eyes then point out yours. Talk about how you are the sameand different.
Pretend to milk a cow (or
goat). Take your dominant
hand, make it into a fist,
relax and repeat.
Head, shoulders, knees and toes
Head, shoulders, knees and toes
Eyes and ears
And mouth and nose
Head, shoulders, knees and toes
Point out the body parts as you
say them. Start slow and get
faster as you get used to them.
Cheek chin cheek chin
