# Two Little Dickie Birds

Two little dickie birds, sitting on a wall,
*Hold up two index fingers*
One named Peter, one named Paul.
*Wiggle one finger for Peter, then the other for Paul*
Fly away Peter, fly away Paul.
*Hide index fingers behind back*
Come back Peter, come back Paul.
*Bring back index fingers one at a time*
Variation
Two little bunnies bouncing on a hill
One named Jenny one named Jill.
Bounce away Jenny, bounce away Jill.
Bounce back Jenny, bounce back Jill.
