# Ex. = Running

-Respond to quarter, eighth and half notes
patterns thru movement.
-Understand the concept of a
musical rest.
-notice and respond to repeated rhythmic
patterns through movement.
-Identify rhythmic patterns that are the
same or different .
-Clap and perform rhythmic
patterns.
-Identify musical tempos
in terms of fast and slow.
-Identify changes in tempo.
-Enact through movement
changes in tempo.
-Perform tempo changes on
instruments and through
movement.
-Identify and move to meter
changes in a musical example.
