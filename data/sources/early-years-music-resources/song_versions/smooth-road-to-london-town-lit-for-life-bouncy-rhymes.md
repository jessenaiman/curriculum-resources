# Smooth Road to London Town

Smooth roads to London town,
Smooth roads to London town
Some are up and some are down
Smooth roads to London town
But by and by we came to a wood
And there the roads are not so good
Bumpy roads, bumpy roads
Bumpy roads to London town.
But by and by we came to the dell
And there the roads are not so swell
Rough road, rough roads
Rough roads to London town
