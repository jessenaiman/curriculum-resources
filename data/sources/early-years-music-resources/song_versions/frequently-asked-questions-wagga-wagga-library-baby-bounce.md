# Frequently Asked Questions

When to arrive? Give yourself and baby enough time to be ready by
10:30 or so. You can enter either via the lagoon door, which leads you
straight to the children's area, or through the main library doors on level 1,
then take the lift down to level 0.
How long does it go for? About 25-30 mins. There is plenty of time and
space afterwards to stretch, mingle, have a snack, browse books, talk
about memberships, and join the library.
What to bring? You don't need to bring anything, but a baby rug can be
helpful for tummy time.
How to book? The program is designed to be experienced as a series of
four different sessions, come to as many as works for you. For the best
experience, you will need one lap per baby. For parents of multiples,
bring along extra adults if you can. Book your free place/s via our What's
On page. Tickets are available one week before each session.
