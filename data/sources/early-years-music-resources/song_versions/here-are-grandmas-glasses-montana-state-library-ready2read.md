# Here are Grandma’s Glasses

Here are Grandma’s glasses.
Here is Grandma’s cap.
This is how she folds her hands,
And puts them in her lap.
And here are Grandpa’s glasses,
And here is Grandpa’s hat,
And this is how he folds his arms -
Just like that!
