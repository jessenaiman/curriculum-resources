# Everybody Line Up

Everybody line up, line up, line up,
everybody line up now.
Stand in front or stand behind,
everybody,
that is how we make a line.
Everybody come and find
your place in line and I’ll find mine.
Everybody come and blend
into the front, the middle and the end.
Everybody’s lined up, lined up, lined up.
Everybody’s lined up now.
