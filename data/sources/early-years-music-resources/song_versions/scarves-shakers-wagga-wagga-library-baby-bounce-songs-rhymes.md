# Scarves & Shakers

Use scarves and shakers to help with fine motor skills like
holding, dropping, and waving.
Play peek-a-boo and tickling games to teach new words
and sounds.
Gently touch your baby with the scarf or shaker while
naming body parts to improve vocabulary.
