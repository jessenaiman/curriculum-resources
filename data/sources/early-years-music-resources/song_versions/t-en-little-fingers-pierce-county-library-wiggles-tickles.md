# T en Little Fingers

(Sing to the tune of “Bumpin’ Downtown
in my Little Red Wagon”)
T wo little hands, ten little fingers,
T wo little hands, ten little fingers,
T wo little hands, ten little fingers,
Count them all with me…
1, 2, 3 little fingers,
4, 5, 6 little fingers,
7 , 8, 9 little fingers,
And one more makes 10.
Dos Manitas,


**Printed actions:**
Hold up two fists, then open hands
