# Uncle John went

to town one by one.
Mother fell off.
And Father fell off.
But Uncle John went on and on
and on and on and on!
(Bounce child on lap,
tipping child to side on “off,”
and lots of bounces on last line.)
Criss-cross applesauce,
(Draw an X on child’s back
with finger.)
Spiders running up your back.
Cool breeze,
(Blow gently on child’s neck
and back of head.)
Tight squeeze,
Now you’ve got the shivers!


**Printed actions:**
Walk fingers up child’s back.
Give child a big hug.
Tickle child gently all over.
