# Hickory-Dickory Dock

Hickory dickory dock.
The mouse ran up the clock
The clock struck one,
And down he runs
Hickory dickory dock
Hokey Pokey Penny a Lump
Hokey pokey, penny a lump
Up & down, up & down, bumpity- bump
If you jump, you're sure to fall
Hokey pokey, that is all!
