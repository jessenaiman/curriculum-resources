Singing songs, chanting rhymes, and sharing stories are
great ways to bond with your baby, while introducing new
vocabulary words. And if a baby likes something, keep
singing/reading it! Repeating the same songs and
rhymes over and over again helps a child learn!
Here are some rhymes and songs XFMPWFVTJOH. Use this
brochure to create bonding moments outside of the
library, such as changing diapers, grocery shopping, and/
or bath time! THIS LITTLE PIGGY

This little piggy stayed home
