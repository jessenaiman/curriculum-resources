# NO MORE MONKEYS JUMPING ON THE BED!

Here is a Hill
Here is a hill all covered with snow.
We’ll take our sleds up.
and ZOOM down we’ll go


**Printed actions:**
Put one hand on hip and make it “snow” with the other hand
Walk fingers up the arm on the hip
Fly fingers quickly down arm
