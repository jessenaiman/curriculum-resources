# Hurry Scurry Little Mouse

Hurry scurry little mouse,
Starts down at your toes.
Hurry scurry little mouse,
Past your knees he goes.
Hurry scurry little mouse,
Past where your tummy is.
Hurry scurry little mouse,
Gives you a mousy kiss.


**Printed actions:**
touch child’s toes
touch child’s knees
touch child’s tummy
give child loud kiss
