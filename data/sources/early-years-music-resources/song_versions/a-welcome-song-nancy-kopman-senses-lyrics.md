# A Welcome Song

Let’s all sing a welcome song,
a song for you and me.
Hear the words and sing along,
it’s all about how we belong.
Let’s all sing a welcome song
about a good place to be.
Clap your hands and sing along with
me.
I belong, you belong, she belongs, he
belongs, they belong, we belong you
see.
I belong, you belong, she belongs, he
belongs, they belong, we all belong you
see.
Oh, let’s all sing a welcome song,
a song that we all can sing.
Sing along ‘cause that’s the way
we can all feel welcome as we all say:
“Welcome to this brand new day.”
