# Shakers: Shake and Stop

Oh you shake and you shake and you shake and you stop.  Shake ‘em up high, shake ‘em down low, shake
them on your tummy, and way down to your toes.
Shakers: Shake your shaker
Shake your shaker near and far, near and far, near and far
Shake your shaker near and far, shake your shaker.


**Printed actions:**
repeat 3x
tune: London Bridge
Shake your shaker high and low… fast and slow… front and back… soft and loud…
