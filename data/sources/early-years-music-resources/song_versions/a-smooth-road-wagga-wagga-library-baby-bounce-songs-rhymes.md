# A Smooth Road

A smooth road, a smooth road,
a smooth road, a smooth road
*Gentle swaying*
A bumpy road, a bumpy road,
a bumpy road, a bumpy road
*Gentle bounces*
A rough road, a rough road,
a rough road, a rough road
*Big bounces*
A hole!!!
*Gently and quickly lower baby*
