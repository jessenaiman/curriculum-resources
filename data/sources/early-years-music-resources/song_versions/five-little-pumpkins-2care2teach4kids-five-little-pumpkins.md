# Five Little Pumpkins

Five Little Pumpkins,
Sitting on the gate.
The first one said,
"Oh my, it's getting late."
The second one said,
"There are stars in the air."
The third one said,
"But we don't care."
The fourth one said,
"Let's run and run!"
The fifth one said,
"I'm ready for some fun."
Oooooo, went the wind
And out went the light.
And the five little pumpkins rolled out of sight.
