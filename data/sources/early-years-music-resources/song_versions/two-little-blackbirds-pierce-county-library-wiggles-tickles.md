# Two Little Blackbirds

Two little blackbirds
Sitting on a hill,
One named Jack and
One named Jill,
Fly away Jack,
Fly away Jill,


**Printed actions:**
Crook index fingers
Put one finger behind your back
Put the other finger behind your back
