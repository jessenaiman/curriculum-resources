# Slowly, Slowly

Slowly, slowly, very slowly creeps the garden snail
Slowly, slowly, very slowly up the garden rail
*Slowly walk fingers up baby's tummy*
Quickly, quickly very quickly runs the little mouse
Quickly, quickly very quickly round about the house
*Lots of tummy tickles*
So Early in the Morning
This is the way we clap our hands,
clap our hands, clap our hands
This is the way we clap our hands so early in the morning.
*Clap baby’s hands*
This is the way we kick our feet,
kick our feet, kick our feet
This is the way we kick our feet so early in the morning.
*Kick baby’s feet*
This is the way we wave our arms,
wave our arms, wave our arms
This is the way we wave our arms so early in the morning.
*Wave baby’s arms*
