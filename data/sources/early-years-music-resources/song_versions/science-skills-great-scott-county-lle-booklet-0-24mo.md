# Science Skills:

Use your child's name instead of Old Macdonald
Try singing a book - there are many book versions of favorite nursery
rhymes and songs!
Stop just before the end of phrases in favorite songs – your child can
try joining in with little noises now!
Babies and toddlers are natural scientists. Let them use all senses to
explore the world around them. As they grow older let them use
objects as tools. Can they hit two blocks together?
