# When I was One, I’d Just Begun

When I was one, I’d just begun
On the day I went to sea,
I jumped aboard a pirate ship
And the captain said to me-
“We’re going this way, that way,
Forwards and backwards,
Over the deep blue sea.
Something yum to fill my tum
And that’s the life for me.”
(Repeat with: When I was two, I lost my shoe…
When I was three, I climbed a tree…
When I was four, I knocked at the door ...
When I was five, I learned to dive…)
