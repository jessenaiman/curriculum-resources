# Storytime Resources

What does successful programming look like?
• Promotes the library’s collection and resources
• Develop early literacy and family literacy skills
• Celebrate diversity
• Develop lifelong learners
• Instill a love of reading, language, and literacy
Things to Think About Before Planning a Storytime
• Age and audience
• Size of the group
• Connection to early literacy
• Your comfort with the materials
• Your presentation style
