# The Grandfather Clock

The Grandfather clock goes Tick---tock---tick---tock
*slowly sway sideways*
The kitchen clock goes Tick-tock-tick-tock
*sway a bit faster*
And mama’s little watch goes Ticketyticketytickety
*sway quickly then cuddle*
