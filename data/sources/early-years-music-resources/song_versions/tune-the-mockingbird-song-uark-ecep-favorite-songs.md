# (Tune: The Mockingbird Song)

I have a dog and his name is Rags
When he eats too much his tummy drags
His ears flip flop
and his tail wig-wags
And when he walks
he zigs and zags


**Printed actions:**
Hold stomach
Wave hands over head
Wave hands behind back
Place hands on hips
Move hips from side to side
