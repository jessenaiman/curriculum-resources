# The Dog Went to Dover

Leg over leg,
The dog went to Dover. (and move the legs in rhythm with the
rhyme)
When he came to a wall,
Then he jumped right over. (on the “jumped, ” lift up both legs
together like
you are making them jump over the wall)


**Printed actions:**
with your child on your lap hold on to the ankles
