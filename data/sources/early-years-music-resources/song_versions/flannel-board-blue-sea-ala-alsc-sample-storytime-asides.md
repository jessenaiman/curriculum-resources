# Flannel Board: Blue Sea

Children retell story using flannel board.
[Optional] Action Rhyme: There Was a Little Turtle
There was a little turtle who lived in a box.
He swam in puddles and climbed on rocks.
He snapped at a mosquito; he snapped at a flea,
He snapped at a minnow; and he snapped at me.
He caught the mosquito; he caught the flea,
He caught the minnow; but he didn’t catch me.
Book: Where’s Spot? by Eric Hill or Wolf’s Chicken Stew by Keiko Kasza
Where’s Spot? In this book Spot’s mother is trying to find him. Let’s see what happens.
