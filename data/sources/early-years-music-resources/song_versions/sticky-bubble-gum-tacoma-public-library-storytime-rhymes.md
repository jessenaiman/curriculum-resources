# Sticky Bubble Gum

Sticky, sticky, sticky bubblegum, bubblegum, bubblegum
Sticky, sticky, sticky bubblegum, sticking to our heads*
Ready, and we pull, and we pull and we pull! Unstuck!
*repeat with different body parts
Five Fat Peas in a Peapod Pressed
Five fat peas in a peapod pressed
One grew, two grew, so did all the rest
They grew and they grew and they never stopped
They grew and grew until the peapod popped!
