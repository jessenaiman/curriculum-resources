# TALK Together:

social Skills:
Take turns naming common items. Build on what your child says. Ifyour toddler names a cup, describe it's color, shape, or function. When asking a question, give your child five seconds to respond.They're strengthening synapses in their brain each time you ask aquestion and they respond.
