# Five Little Speckled Frogs

Five little speckled frogs
Sat on a speckled log
eating a most delicious bug
Yum, yum, yum
One jumped into the pool
where it was nice and cool,
Then there were four green speckled frogs
Glub, Glub, Glub
Sing down to 0 frogs - last line then is:
Glub, Glub, Glub - G - L - U - B
Jack in the Box
Jack-in-the-box, so quiet and still
won’t you come out and play with me?
Of course I will!


**Printed actions:**
Hold up and bop five fingers
Pick off and eat bug
Rub tummy
One finger motions hopping into pool
Hold up four fingers
deep voice
Crouch on the floor, very small
Pop up, arms stretched up
