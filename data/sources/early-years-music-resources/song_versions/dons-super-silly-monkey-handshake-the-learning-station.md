# Don’s Super Silly Monkey Handshake

As you shake a friend’s hand share a greeting such as “Hello” or
“Good Morning”. Share this greeting in another language. Here is
“Hello” in a variety of other languages:
Hallo - German Hola - Spanish Bonjour - French Namaste - Hindi
Ciào - Italian Salut - Romanian Hoezit  - South African
Aloha - Hawaiian Jambo - Swahili Mambo - Congo
Konnichiha -  Japanese Make new friends,
but keep the old.
One is silver,
and the other gold.
A circle is round
it has no end.
That’s how long,
I will be your friend.
Form a circle, hold hands and chant this friendship
song as you shake your neighbor’s hands.


**Printed actions:**
pronounced howzit
pronounced as konnichiwa
