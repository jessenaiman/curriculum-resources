# These are Baby’s Fingers

These are baby’s fi ngers,
These are baby’s toes,
This is baby’s belly button,
Round and round it goes!
Round and Round the Garden
Round and round the garden,
Goes the teddy bear.
One step, two steps,
Tickle you under there.
Round and round the haystack,
Goes the little mouse.
One step, two steps,
Into his little house.
Let’s Go to the Forest
Let’s go to the forest.
What will we do there? (point to Mother - could be mother, father
Find our mother. or grandmother, etc.)
What will we do with her?
Kiss her, kiss her, kiss her!
Bumblebee
Bumblebee was in the barn
Carrying his dinner under his arm
Bzzzzzzzzzzzzz…zt!
