# THE JINGLE SONG

move shaker egg or jingle bells to the
directions in the lyrics
We jingle up high,
We jingle down low,
We jingle very fast,
And we jingle very slow,
We jingle to the ceiling,
We jingle to the ground,
We jingle and we jangle
As we dance all around!
