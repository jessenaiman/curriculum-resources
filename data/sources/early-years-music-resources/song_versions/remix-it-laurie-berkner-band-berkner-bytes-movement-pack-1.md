# Remix It!

Berkner BytesBerkner BytesBerkner Bytes
Have children sit on the floor and
assume a “boat pose” (Navasana yoga
pose) with feet lifted slightly off the
ground. As they go on the "hayride"
they can bounce their bodies up and
down.
On "I see apple trees and I say hello,"
cue children to wave to a peer.
This movement activity helps to build
core strength, which is important as
children learn to sit in chairs and write.
For the winter, sing “Sleigh Ride” in
place of “Hayride.”
