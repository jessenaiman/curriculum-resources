# Compiled by Pierce County Library System

Grow a
reader!
Here’s a Ball for Baby
Here’s a ball for baby,
Big and soft and round.
Here is baby’s hammer,
See how he can pound.
Here’s is baby’s trumpet,
Tootle, tootle, too
Here is baby’s favorite game


**Printed actions:**
Pound one fist on the other
Hold up two hands touching fingertips to form ball
Form trumpet with hand
Cover your eyes with hands open
