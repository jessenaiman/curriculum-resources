# Cuckoo! Cuckoo!

(Toddlers and preschoolers can act this rhyme out standing up as
well and jumping up on “cuckoo”)
T wo Little Hands,


**Printed actions:**
This time, lift baby up gently twice
Repeat at least once more lifting baby three times this time.
A great rhyme to pair with the nursery rhyme “Hickory Dickory Dock.”
