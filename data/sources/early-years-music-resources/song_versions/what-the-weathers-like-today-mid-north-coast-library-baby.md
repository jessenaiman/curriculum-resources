# What The Weather’s Like Today

Look outside now, can you say
What the weather’s like today?
Is there sunshine, is there rain,
Is wind blowing down the lane?
Are there snowflakes falling down?
Are there big clouds floating round?
Look outside now, can you say
What the weather’s like today?
