# Why Board Books?

Board books are fantastic for babies for several reasons:
Durable: Thick pages withstand rough handling.
Safe: Rounded edges and non-toxic materials.
Engaging: Bright pictures and simple text.
Interactive: Textures, flaps, and mirrors.
Easy to Handle: Perfect size for little hands.
