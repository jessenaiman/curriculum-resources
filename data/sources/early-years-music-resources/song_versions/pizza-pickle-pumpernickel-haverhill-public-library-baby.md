# Pizza, Pickle, Pumpernickel

Pizza, pickle, pumpernickel,
My little baby deserves a tickle! Tickle
One for her nose, and one for her toes,
And one for her belly where the hot dog goes! Tickle
Zoom, Zoom, Zoom
Zoom, zoom, zoom, Rock baby side to side
We’re going to the moon.
Do you want to take a trip? Bounce baby
Climb aboard my rocket ship!
Zoom, zoom, zoom, Rock baby side to side
We’re going to the moon,
In 5-4-3-2-1 Bounce baby
BLAST OFF! Lift baby into the air
