# I’m a Little Teapot

I’m a little teapot, short and stout.
Here is my handle;
Here is my spout.
When I get all steamed up,
Hear me shout.
Tip me over and pour me out.
(Bend over at waist to the side
and then stand upright again.)
I’m a special teapot, it is true.
Here, let me show you
what I can do.
I can change my handle
and my spout.
Tip me over and pour me out.
Jack in the Box
Jack in the box, you sit so still.
(Kneel on floor with head covered
by arms or hands.)
Won’t you come out? Yes, I will!


**Printed actions:**
Put one hand on hip.
Put other hand up in the air.
Switch positions of arms.
Bend to the side at waistline.
Pop up on last phrase.
