# Additonal Verses:

A cow, a cow, a cow got on a bus
And all, and all, and all the people said,
"Mooooove over!“ Move in circle
-A sheep …"Baaaack up!“ Lean back
-A frog … “We’re hoppy to see you!” Big bounces -
-A cat … “Your tail’s tickling us!” Tickle child
