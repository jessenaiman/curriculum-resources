# Five Monkeys

Five little monkeys swinging from a tree.
Teasing Mr. Crocodile, “You can’t catch me,”
Along came Mr. Crocodile quietly as can be.
Then SNAP,
Then there were four little monkeys swinging from a tree.
Teasing Mr. Crocodile, “You can’t catch me.”
Then there were no more monkeys swinging from the tree.


**Printed actions:**
Swing finger as if teasing
Arm becomes the crocodile
Two arms together as if crocodiles mouth
