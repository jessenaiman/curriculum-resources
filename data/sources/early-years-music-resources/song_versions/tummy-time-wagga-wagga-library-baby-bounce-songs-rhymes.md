# Tummy Time

Engage with Pictures: Point to and describe pictures to
keep them interested.
Short Sessions: Start with a few minutes and gradually
increase the time.
Comfortable Surface: Ensure your baby is on a soft, safe
surface.
Use Board Books: Place them in front of your baby during
tummy time to encourage lifting their head.
