# Five Little Ducks

Five little ducks went out to play.
Over the hills and far away.
Mother duck called quack, quack, quack.
Four little ducks came waddling back
Four little ducks . . .
Three little ducks . . .
Two little ducks . . .
One little duck . . .
Sad mother duck . . .
And all of the 5 little ducks came back.
