# I’m a Little Teapot

I’m a little teapot,
Short and stout -
Here is my handle,
Here is my spout.
When I get all steamed up,
Hear me SHOUT -
“Tip me over and pour me out!”
I can change my handle,
I can change my spout -
Tip me over and pour me out!


**Printed actions:**
one hand on hip
bend other arm out to side
rock from side to side
tilt body to “spout” side
reverse sides for handle and spout
