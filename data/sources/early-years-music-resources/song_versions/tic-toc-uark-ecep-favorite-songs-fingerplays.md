# Tic, Toc

Tic, Toc, Tic, Toc (Child may go side to side on parents lap or may
I’m a little cuckoo clock! “tic toc” on their own with feet touching. On final
Tic, Toc, Tic, Toc “cuckoo” you roll backward and come up again)
And now striking one o’clock:
Cuckoo!


**Printed actions:**
two o’clock would be two cuckoos, etc.
