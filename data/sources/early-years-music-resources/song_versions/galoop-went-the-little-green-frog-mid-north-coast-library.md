# Galoop Went The Little Green Frog

Galoop went the little green frog one day,
Galoop went the little green frog,
Galoop went the little green frog one day,
And his eyes went glub, glub, glub!
But, we all know frogs go  la-dee-da-dee-da!
La-dee-da-dee-da!  La-dee-da-dee-da!
We all know frogs go  la-dee-da-dee-da!
They don’t go glub, glub, glub!
