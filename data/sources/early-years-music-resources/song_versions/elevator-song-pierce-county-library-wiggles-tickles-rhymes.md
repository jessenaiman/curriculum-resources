# Elevator Song

Oh, the city is great and the city is grand
There are lots of tall buildings
On a little piece of land
We live way up on the fifty-seventh floor
And this is what we do
When we go out the door
Take the elevator up,
Take the elevator down,
Take the elevator up,
Take the elevator down,
Take the elevator up,
Take the elevator down,
Then we spin around!

23

Lift your child up in the air
Gently lower them down
Lift up in the air
Gently lower them down
Lift up in the air for the last time
Gently lower them down
Spin gently around or give them a hug
