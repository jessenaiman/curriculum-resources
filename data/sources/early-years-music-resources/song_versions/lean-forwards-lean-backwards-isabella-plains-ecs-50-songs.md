# Lean Forwards, Lean Backwards

Lean forwards, lean backwards
To the left, to the right
Stretch up stretch down
To the left, to the right
With a stomp and a slap,
And a clap and a click
Now do it backwards to see if it sticks
With stomp and slap
And a clap and a click
Now do it backwards to see if it sticks
