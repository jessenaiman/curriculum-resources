# I Feel Happy

I feel happy,
I feel free.
I have big bright feelings inside me.
My heart’s jumping
with energy.
Feeling happy is so good for me!
What makes me happy?
What makes my day?
What gets me feeling this way?
I might feel happy
a short time or long,
but when I feel this
I sing a happy song!
Feeling happy
makes me glad
to have felt these feelings that I’ve had.
I feel happy,
I feel free.
I want you to
feel happy with me!
