# Warm Kitty, Soft Kitty

(In this rhyme, have the child pretend that this is
their first kitty or puppy and pet it gently. This is a
great way to teach gentle touch for household pets.)
Warm kitty, soft kitty,
Little ball of fur
Pretty kitty, sleepy kitty.
Purr, purr, purr .
Warm puppy, soft puppy,
Little ball of fluff
Happy puppy, sleepy puppy.
Ruff, ruff, ruff
