# Poor Humpty Dumpty!

Row your Boat
Row row row your boat, gently down the stream. Merrily merrily merrily merrily, life is but a dream.
Popcorn
Popcorn, popcorn in a pan, Shake’em up, shake’em up, Bam, bam, bam
Zoom down the free way
Zoom down the freeway, Zoom down the freeway, Zoom down the freeway, Fast!
Up goes the drawbridge, Up goes the drawbridge, The boat is going past.
Down goes the drawbridge, Down goes the drawbridge. The boat has passed at last.
Zoom down the freeway, Zoom down the freeway, Zoom down the freeway, EXIT!
The Noble Duke of York
The noble Duke of York, He had ten thousand men,
He marched them up to the top of the hill And he marched them down again
And when you’re up, you’re up, And when you’re down, you’re down
And when you’re only halfway up, You’re neither up nor down!
He marched them to the left, He marched them to the right
He even marched them upside down, Now wasn’t that a sight!


**Printed actions:**
hold child facing you, holding hands, and row back and forth
Also can be used as a parachute song
or Grand Old Duke…
Child sits on parent’s knees, move knees up and down, left and right with soldiers
