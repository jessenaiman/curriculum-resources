# The Letters”

How to use the song:
Let’s go looking for letters! Give
each child one letter to find printed
in the classroom. Students may
look at labels, book titles, or
posters.
For the length of the song, students
will move about the classroom to
find their assigned letter.
This activity can also be adapted as a book
hunt. Have students look for one letter in a
book. If your curriculum encourages letter of
the week, this could be a weekly routine for
each letter!
