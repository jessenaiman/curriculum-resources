# Pitter Patter

When rain starts falling, it comes down softly and slowly . . .
The rain is falling down
Splash!
The rain is falling down
Splash!
Pitter, patter, pitter, patter
Pitter, patter, pitter, patter
Then the rain gets louder and faster
Sometimes we hear thunder
Hear the thunder
Crash!
Hear the thunder
Crash!
Thunder! Thunder!
Thunder! Thunder!
Then the storm starts to stop.
The thunder stops and the rain slowly stops and it is quiet.
14


**Printed actions:**
Wiggle fingers down
Pat knees
Wiggle fingers down
Pat knees
Tap knees alternately
Tap knees alternately
Repeat above louder and faster
Hand to ear
Clap hands loudly
Hand to ear
Clap hands loudly
Stamp feet fast
Stamp feet fast
Repeat first verse slowly and softly
