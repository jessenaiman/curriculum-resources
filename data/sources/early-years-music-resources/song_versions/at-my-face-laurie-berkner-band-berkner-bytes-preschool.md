# At My Face”

Pair this song with an emotions
guessing game. Have students guess
what emotion the teacher is
communicating with their face.
Have students hold a scarf in front of
their faces. On "Just take a look at my
face," have the students pull down
their scarves to reveal the emotion on
their faces.
