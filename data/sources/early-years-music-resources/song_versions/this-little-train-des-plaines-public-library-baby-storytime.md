# THIS LITTLE TRAIN

This little train ran up the track
It went choo! choo!
And then it ran back
The other little train ran up the track
It went choo! choo!
And then it ran back.
wiggles and tickles


**Printed actions:**
walk fingers up baby’s arm or leg
tap baby’s nose
walk fingers back down baby’s arm
