# Up, Down, Turn Around

Up, down, turn around,
Touch the sky, touch the ground,
Wiggle fingers, wiggle toes,
Wiggle shoulders, say hello.
Up, down, turn around,
Touch the sky, touch the ground,
Jiggle tummy, blink your eyes,
Blow a kiss and say good-bye!
