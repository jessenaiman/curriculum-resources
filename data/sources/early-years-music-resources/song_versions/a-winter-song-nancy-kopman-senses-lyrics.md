# A Winter Song

Winter is a time of year when we
can sing about the changes we might
see.
It’s different from the Spring, Summer
and Fall.
Winter’s the last season of them all.
In some parts of the world, Winter is
cold.
Icicles and frost and winter clothes.
Snowflakes are falling down to the
ground.
Everything’s white when you look
around.
Socks, boots and sweaters
coats, snow pants-
shovelling the snow so you can walk.
Scarves, hats and earmuffs,
mittens on your hands.
See breath in the air when people talk
.
Winter is a time when we can sing
about the magic that nature can bring.
Sing for the season, sing for what you
see.
Sing a Winter song along with me
.
Sing a Winter song and feel happy
.
Happy Winter, everybody (happy
Winter)!


**Printed actions:**
you can walk in the snow
when they talk
happy Winter
happy Winter
