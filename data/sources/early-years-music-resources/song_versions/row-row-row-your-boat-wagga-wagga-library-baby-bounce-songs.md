# Row, Row, Row Your Boat

Row, row, row your boat,
Gently down the stream
Merrily, Merrily, Merrily, Merrily
Life is but a dream
Rock, rock, rock your boat,
Sway from side to side
Merrily, Merrily, Merrily, Merrily
What a bumpy ride
