# Where is Thumbkin?

Tune of: Fr re Jacques or Are You Sleeping
Where is thumbkin?
Where is thumbkin?
Here I am
Here I am
How are you today sir?
Very well, I thank you
Run away
Run away
Additional verses:
Where is pointer?
Where is tall man?
Where is ring man?
Where is pinkie?
Where is whole family?
All fingerplays and action songs are "Traditional" unless otherwise noted.


**Printed actions:**
Start with hands behind back
Bring right thumb to front
Bring left thumb to front
Wiggle thumbs as if they are "talking" to each other
Hide right hand behind back
Hide left hand behind back
