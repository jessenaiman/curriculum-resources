# All I Want are Hugs

All I want are hugs from you, (hold your little one in your arms and
Hugs to last the whole day through. give a gentle hug every time
you say
Hugs just make me feel so good, the word “hug”)
So hug me, hug me, hug me.
Give me lots of hugs today,
Hugs to chase the blues away.
Hugs to last the whole day through,
Just hug me, hug me, hug me!
