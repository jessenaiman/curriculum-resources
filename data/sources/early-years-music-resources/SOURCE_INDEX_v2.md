# SOURCE INDEX v2 — Early-Years Music Song-Teaching Resources

**Compiled:** 2026-08-04 (round 2 in progress)
**Purpose:** Index of freely shared, openly downloadable early-years music teaching
resources, now including historical/public-domain songbooks so the collection can
show **how songs were taught over time**. Each saved document is one *version* of
how a song was taught; the year/era/region metadata fields (v2 schema, see
`RESEARCH_RULES_v2.md`) support the historical comparison.

**Collection rules:** Free/openly published/public-domain material only. No
paywalled, login-only, or restricted content.
