Looking for more in Bookbug Song and Rhyme Library?

Cross, Cross, Line, Line (video)

Song type: Actions, Active, Face to face, Rhymes, Tickling
Language: English
Age group: 0-2, 3-5
Audience: Baby, Children, Pre-school, Toddler
Topics: Bookbug

How to use this song with babies

Following the words of the rhyme, draw on your baby’s back. Give a gentle blow on their neck (cool breeze), a lovely cuddle (tight squeeze), then end with some tickles!

How to use this song with toddlers

Add some excitement and anticipation at the end by pausing before the ‘tickles’ - your child will get so excited when they realise what’s coming next!

How to use this song with 3 to 5-year olds

Children can do the actions to each other (in pairs or around the circle), or to a teddy bear or soft toy - or even to you!

Cross, Cross, Line, Line lyrics

Cross, cross, line, line

Spiders crawling up your spine.

Cool breeze... tight squeeze...

And now you've got the shivers!
