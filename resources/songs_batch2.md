## Methodology Notes

1. **Website Research:** Fetched album pages from https://kathyreidnaiman.com/ and individual track pages
2. **Lyrics Analysis:** Extracted lyrics with embedded action instructions (text in square brackets)
3. **Database Cross-reference:** Matched songs from curriculum.db with website track listings
4. **Knowledge Supplementation:** For songs not found on website or without lyrics, used knowledge of traditional children's songs and Kathy Reid-Naiman's teaching style
5. **Bilingual Consideration:** It's a Beautiful Day album contains French/English bilingual songs; both versions noted

## Key Patterns Observed

- **Lullabies** (Rock a Bye Baby, Baby's Boat, Prairie Lullaby): Typically involve gentle rocking, cradling, swaying
- **Action songs** (Duke of York, Jump Jim Joe): Involve gross motor movements, formation changes
- **Fingerplays** (Come Look a See, Wiggle Your Fingers): Focus on fine motor skills, body parts
- **Animal songs** (Pterodactyl, Old Bald Eagle): Involve imitation of animal movements
- **Seasonal songs** (Heigh Ho Halloween, A Chubby Little Snowman): Tied to calendar events
- **French bilingual songs**: Common in It's a Beautiful Day album, often with cultural dance elements
