## Research Notes

1. **Primary Source:** kathyreidnaiman.com was used as the primary source for album track listings and general descriptions. The website provides album-level information but limited per-song details.

2. **Supplementary Sources:** The verified reference file (`kathy-reid-naiman-songs-verified.md`) was used for songs that appeared in multiple albums, providing cross-referenced action and topic data.

3. **Traditional Song Knowledge:** For songs not fully documented on the website, actions and movements were based on:
   - Traditional children's song performance practices
   - Kathy Reid-Naiman's known teaching approach (Pete Seeger tradition)
   - Developmental appropriateness for each age group
   - Standard movements associated with these traditional songs

4. **Topic Assignment:** Topics were assigned based on:
   - Primary educational content of the song
   - Physical movements and actions required
   - Developmental goals addressed
   - Curriculum mapping categories (Animals, Seasons, Body Parts, etc.)

5. **Age Groups:** Age recommendations follow Kathy's own classifications where available, supplemented by developmental guidelines:
   - Infants (6-18 months): Simple sensory, lullabies, fingerplays
   - Toddlers (1-3 years): Movement songs, animal sounds, basic concepts
   - Preschoolers (3-5 years): Complex actions, counting, storytelling
   - Early Elementary (5-7 years): Extended songs, cultural content, coordination
