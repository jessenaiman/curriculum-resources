# Early Years Nature & Science Songs

A curated collection of North American folk and educational nature/science songs for infants through Kindergarten, organized by age band and science topic.

---

## INFANT (0–18 months)

### 🐾 Animals

#### Baby Beluga
- **Artist:** Raffi
- **Album:** Baby Beluga (1980)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Marine animals, ocean habitat, beluga whale life
- **Activity Description:** Sing gently while doing slow hand motions mimicking a swimming whale—both arms together moving up and down like fins. Infants can be bounced on laps to the gentle melody. Use a white balloon or soft whale plush as a visual prop. For sensory extension, create a blue water bin with a small toy whale.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.singup.org/free-resources/baby-beluga

#### Here Comes the Sun
- **Artist:** Raffi (cover); also popular in traditional folk form
- **Album:** Dog on the Floor / Rise and Shine (various recordings)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Day/night cycle, sun rising, seasonal change (spring after winter)
- **Activity Description:** Start with hands covering face (representing darkness/night), then slowly open arms wide to "see" the sun come up. Use a warm yellow blanket or fabric to simulate sunrise. Sing softly at morning transition time each day.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eTMFvKdkr_o

### 🌧️ Weather

#### Lullaby, Lullaby
- **Artist:** Kathy Reid-Naiman
- **Album:** A Smooth Road to London Town (2002)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Nighttime environment, stars, moon; introduces concepts of weather being unpredictable ("Things go right, things go wrong")
- **Activity Description:** Gentle knee bounce song. Rock baby gently while singing; use slow, soothing movements appropriate for sleep time. Point out moon or stars through a window if visible.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge / Merriweather Records; no public lyrics page found

### 💧 Water

#### Blue Whale Lullaby
- **Artist:** Fran McKinney (used widely in infant music classes; adopted by Kathy Reid-Naiman programs)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Ocean animals, size comparison (biggest animal on Earth), underwater environment
- **Activity Description:** Slow knee-bounce lullaby. Make large sweeping arm motions to represent a big whale's tail. Very calming tempo suitable for wind-down time.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://franmckinney.com/friends.htm

## TODDLER (18 months–3 years)

### 🐾 Animals

#### Little Seed
- **Artist:** Elizabeth Mitchell (singing Woody Guthrie)
- **Album:** Little Seed: Songs for Children by Woody Guthrie (2012)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Plant growth, seed planting, life cycle of plants
- **Activity Description:** Curled-up crouch motion for the "seed," slowly rise up while opening arms wide as the seed grows. Can be paired with actual bean seed germination activity—give each child soaked beans to observe roots and shoots developing over several days.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://woodyguthrie.org/Lyrics/Little_Seed.htm

#### Over in the Meadow
- **Traditional Folk Song** (Lillian Moore adaptation of Olive Wadsworth text)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Wildlife habitats, counting, animal young (fawn, calf, kitten, etc.), meadow ecosystem
- **Activity Description:** Traditional finger-counting or toe-counting song—one finger for the mother animal, one more for the baby. Act out each animal movement (leap like a deer, hop like a bunny). Great for circle time.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; published in book *Over in the Meadow* by Olive Wadsworth, illustrated by Lee Wardlaw

#### The Little Green Frog
- **Artist:** Jim Rule / Various (traditional folk melody, "Mmm Ahh Went the Little Green Frog")
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Pond ecosystems, frog classification, amphibian life cycle, sound-making in animals
- **Activity Description:** Crouch low and hop like a frog between verses. Create different frog sounds for different verses. Extend with foam pool noodles painted green for "frog legs." Show pictures of tadpole-to-frog metamorphosis during sing-along.
- **Lyrics URL:** http://www.songsforteaching.com/folk/littlegreenfrog.htm

### 🌧️ Weather

#### Rain and Thunder
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Album:** Sleep Tight! (2018)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Rain vs. thunder as separate but related weather phenomena, high vs. low sounds, piano range mapping
- **Activity Description:** Play high notes on a keyboard (or make high-pitched "tinkling" rain sounds) for rain, low rumbling notes for thunder. Children can shake scarves above their heads for rain, stomp feet for thunder. Use a rainstick instrument to add authentic rain sounds.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### Here Comes the Sun (Toddler Version)
- **Artist:** Raffi
- **Album:** Rise and Shine (1981)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Sunrise, temperature changes, seasons ending, daily cycles
- **Activity Description:** Toddlers stretch upward and reach toward light. Go outside on sunny days to connect the song to real experience. On cloudy days, use a yellow ball to represent the sun coming from behind clouds.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eTMFvKdkr_o

### 🍂 Seasons

#### Break of Dawn
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants (adapted from "She'll Be Coming 'Round the Mountain")
- **Album:** Baby Beatles? (2013) — actually based on a lullaby version
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Morning transition, beginning of day, natural light changes
- **Activity Description:** Slow, lullaby-tempo rendition used as a calming morning or afternoon transition. Stretch arms upward as dawn breaks. Good for winding down nap time.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

### 🌱 Plants

#### Everything Grows
- **Artist:** Raffi
- **Album:** Baby Light, Baby Bright (1976)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Growth process, what living things need (sun, rain, soil), all forms of growth
- **Activity Description:** Fingerplays for each element: curl fingers inward for seeds in dirt, open hands upward for flowers blooming, wave arms for trees growing tall. Pair with classroom gardening—plant actual seeds and sing this song daily as they grow. Raffi's board book edition is available.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; Raffi Foundation resources; YouTube read-aloud: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HbpP_loGIek

#### Grass, Grass, Grass
- **Artist:** Elizabeth Mitchell (singing Woody Guthrie)
- **Album:** Little Seed: Songs for Children by Woody Guthrie (2012)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Grass types, where grass grows, plant identification, outdoor ecosystems
- **Activity Description:** Wiggle fingers like blades of grass swaying in the wind. Walk outside to find different types of grass/grass-like plants. Compare indoor carpet vs. outdoor grass texture.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; album source

### 💧 Water/Nature Sounds

#### Listen and Learn (Sound Song)
- **Artist:** Kathy Reid-Naiman
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Listening skills, identifying environmental sounds, auditory discrimination
- **Activity Description:** Play the track and have children freeze/stop when they hear sounds. Identify which sounds are nature vs. man-made. Encourage children to make animal sounds, rain sounds, wind sounds in response to cues. Develops early scientific observation skills.
- **Lyrics URL:** Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/track/44jhTq2TWyguCUJt0nWJqU

#### Swimming, Swimming in the Swimming Pool
- **Artist:** Kathy Reid-Naiman
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Water properties, buoyancy, aquatic activities
- **Activity Description:** Arm-mimic swimming motions around the room. Alternate between "splashing" (fast movement) and "floating" (slow). Add waterproof toys to water play centers after listening.
- **Lyrics URL:** Spotify listing; general knowledge

## PRESCHOOL (3–5 years)

### 🐾 Animals

#### Animal Songs (Compilation)
- **Artist:** Raffi
- **Album:** Animal Songs (2008) / various Raffi albums including *Baby Beluga*, *Balloons in the Sky*, *Rise and Shine*
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Multiple animal species, animal habitats, animal movement, biodiversity
- **Key Songs:** "Going to the Zoo," "Here Sits a Monkey," "Little People," "Every Raindrop Is a Tiny Star"
- **Activity Description:** Each animal song includes specific movement instructions. "Going to the Zoo" encourages marching/movement. "Here Sits a Monkey" uses body positioning. Combine with stuffed animals and picture books matching each creature discussed.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://raffi.fandom.com/wiki/Animal_Songs

#### Five Little Ducks / Ten in the Bed
- **Traditional Folk/Nursery Rhyme** (performed by Pete Seeger, Raffi, Fred Penner, Dan Zanes)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Counting backward/forward, duck behavior, family grouping, subtraction
- **Activity Description:** Use five duck puppets to model each verse, removing one puppet each chorus. Physical jumping activity for "Ten in the Bed"—start sitting close together and "fall out" with each count-down. Connect to field trips observing ducks at local ponds.
- **Lyrics URL:** Traditional; general knowledge

#### Froggie Went a-Courtin'
- **Traditional English-American Folk Song** (performed by Pete Seeger, Elizabeth Mitchell, Caspar Babypants)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Food chains, pond/wetland ecosystem, animal relationships, predator-prey dynamics
- **Activity Description:** Act out the food chain story—each verse introduces a new predator that catches the previous one. Have children line up and act out who eats whom. Draw the pond food web on a poster. Elizabeth Mitchell's Smithsonian Folkways lesson plan provides structured classroom guidance.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://folkways.si.edu/elizabeth-mitchell/froggie-went-a-courtin/american-folk-childrens/music/track/smithsonian

#### Arm in Arm (The Octopus)
- **Artist:** Elizabeth Mitchell
- **Album:** Blue Clouds (2012)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Animal anatomy (tentacles/arms), counting body parts, marine biology
- **Activity Description:** Groups of four students link arms to represent an octopus's eight arms. Discuss how many legs/have arms humans have compared to other animals. Move like different sea creatures. Extension: invent new verses with different animals (elephant with trunk, bird with wings).
- **Lyrics URL:** https://folkways.si.edu/elizabeth-mitchell/arm-in-arm/american-folk-childrens/music/track/smithsonian

#### The Little Bird
- **Artist:** Elizabeth Mitchell
- **Album:** Blue Clouds (2012)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Bird anatomy, bird song, musical instruments as bird calls
- **Activity Description:** Call-and-response singing pattern. Children imitate bird call-and-response. Identify instruments used (glockenspiel, percussion, guitar) and mimic playing them. Discuss differences between bird flight patterns (owl vs. bat vs. bee as noted in Smithsonian Folkways lesson plan).
- **Lyrics URL:** https://folkways.si.edu/elizabeth-mitchell/the-little-bird/american-folk-childrens/music/track/smithsonian

#### San Toki (Mountain Bunny)
- **Artist:** Elizabeth Mitchell
- **Album:** Blue Clouds (2012)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Mountain habitat, rabbits/coney behavior, Caribbean folk tradition
- **Activity Description:** Bunny hopping movement around the room. Discuss mountain environments and what animals live there. Hops, jumps, and stillness game—"when the bunny is safe, freeze!"
- **Lyrics URL:** https://folkways.si.edu/elizabeth-mitchell/blue-clouds/childrens/music/album/smithsonian

#### Hop Up, My Ladies
- **Artist:** Elizabeth Mitchell
- **Album:** Blue Clouds (2012)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Animal movement, jumping locomotion
- **Activity Activity:** Jumping game—children hop in place. Energetic warm-up song. Can discuss which animals hop (rabbits, frogs, kangaroos).
- **Lyrics URL:** https://folkways.si.edu/elizabeth-mitchell/blue-clouds/childrens/music/album/smithsonian

### 🌧️ Weather

#### I Can Sing a Rainbow
- **Artist:** Fred Penner
- **Album:** Happy Feet / Sing With Fred (2002)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Light refraction/prisms creating rainbows, rainbow color sequence (ROYGBIV), colors in nature after rain
- **Activity Description:** Sing while pointing to or holding up colored cards in rainbow order (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet). Create prism experiments with a glass of water and flashlight to show real rainbows forming. Paint with watercolors after rain. Practice color sequencing.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://genius.com/Fred-penner-i-can-sing-a-rainbow-lyrics

#### When the Rain Stops
- **Traditional / Performed by various artists including Jessica Harper**
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Rain cycle, puddles drying, what happens after rain, weather transitions
- **Activity Description:** Use body percussion to represent rain starting/stopping. March slowly to represent calm after rain. Outside walk immediately after rainfall to look for puddles, worms, rainbows. Sing about what we see.
- **Lyrics URL:** Available via teachearlyyears.com

### 🍂 Seasons

#### Balloons in the Sky
- **Artist:** Raffi
- **Album:** Balloons in the Sky (1983)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Wind, floating objects, air currents, observation of sky/clouds
- **Activity Description:** Children hold lightweight scarves or balloons and move them with "wind" (teacher blowing gently or using paper fans). Discuss why things float or sink. Go outside on windy days to observe leaves, clouds, and debris movement.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; Raffi albums; YouTube performance

#### Every Little Thing
- **Artist:** Raffi
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Nature observation, appreciation of natural world details, mindfulness in nature
- **Activity Description:** "Scavenger hunt" listening activity—close eyes and identify natural sounds heard. Take nature walks and encourage children to notice small details (a bug crawling, a leaf changing color). Builds scientific observation habits.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge

#### Reason for the Seasons
- **Artist:** Raffi
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Four seasons, earth's tilt/orbit, seasonal changes, cyclical patterns in nature
- **Activity Description:** Assign a movement to each season (spin for spring breezes, stretch wide for summer sun, crunch motions for fall leaves, shiver for winter cold). Create a large paper clock showing four seasons around its edge. Children march around it naming seasonal changes they observe.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; Raffi repertoire

### 🌱 Plants/Garden

#### The Garden Song
- **Artist:** Traditionally associated with environmental children's music; appears in multiple teachers' repertoires including Raffi
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Gardening process, soil, compost, plant needs (sunlight, water, nutrients), ecosystems
- **Activity Description:** Act out planting seeds (crouch down), watering (tilt hands downward), watching things grow (slow rise). Create a class garden plot. Visit a community garden. Compost observations—discuss what decomposes and what doesn't.
- **Lyrics URL:** journal.childrensmusic.org discusses these environmental songs in detail

#### Plant a Tree for Tomorrow
- **Artist:** Dianne Baker
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Trees, planting, future thinking, environmental stewardship, carbon/oxygen
- **Activity Description:** Digging motions, placing seeds in ground, patting down soil, waiting, watching grow. Plant a tree or shrub as a class activity. Make handmade bookmarks to mark the tree. Discuss what gives us oxygen.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.songsforteaching.com/environmentnature/plantatree.htm

#### I'm a Bee
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Bees, pollination, insect anatomy, honey production, farm-to-table ecology
- **Activity Description:** Buzzing sounds, wiggling wings, hexagon shapes drawn while dancing. Role-play: one child is a flower, others are bees collecting nectar. Discuss how bees help gardens grow. Make honeycomb craft with brown construction paper hexagons.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### Fuzzy Buzzy Buzzing Bug
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Insects, fuzzy textures, bugs in the environment, insect characteristics
- **Activity Description:** Wiggle and buzz like bees/butterflies. Touch different textured items (real moss, cotton balls, pine cones) and describe whether they're fuzzy, smooth, rough. Outdoor bug hunt with magnifying glasses.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### A Dancing Tree
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Trees, photosynthesis (implicit), seasons affecting trees, tree parts
- **Activity Description:** Whole-body tree role-play: arms as branches swaying, legs as roots planted firmly. Shimmer leaves for summer, drop hands (leaves) for autumn, stand bare for winter, wiggle new "leaves" for spring. Seasonal tree craft with paper plates.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### Watch Petunia Dance
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Flowers, flower names, petal movement, garden plants
- **Activity Description:** Gentle swaying representing flowers in a breeze. Name different flower species. Observe real flowers closely and describe petal shapes/colors. Watercolor painting of favorite flowers.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### Go Slow, Slugger
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Snails/slugs, garden pests, slow vs. fast movement, mollusk biology
- **Activity Description:** Kids move very slowly with shell-back props. Compare speed of slug vs. butterfly vs. ant. Draw snail shells with spiral patterns. Anticipation building for garden exploration.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

### 💧 Water/Ocean

#### Here Is the Sea
- **Artist:** Kathy Reid-Naiman
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Ocean waves, tides, marine ecosystems, water bodies
- **Activity Description:** Large sweeping arm waves moving side to side, growing bigger or quieter based on tempo. Blue tissue paper streamers waved overhead. Sand discovery center with seashells, magnifying glasses, tide pool drawings.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbZrtSzxT8k

#### Row, Row, Row Your Boat
- **Traditional Folk Song** (performed by Fred Penner, Pete Seeger, Dan Zanes, and countless others)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Water movement, streams/rivers flow, friction in water, river-to-ocean journey
- **Activity Description:** Sitting rowing motions, then standing to "row" across the room. Adjust tempo for fast vs. slow water. Discuss current/pushing forces. Add verses: "Grumble, grumble, crocodile!" for engagement. Use blue fabric tunnels as "water passages."
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge / traditional

#### Over the Deep Blue Sea
- **Artist:** Used in early childhood curriculum (e.g., Lena Leon / Early Childhood Music programs)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Ocean travel, ships, waves, water crossings, marine geography
- **Activity Description:** Circular walking game with partners holding hands and arching arms overhead for the ship. Bob up and down simulating wave motion. Discuss boats, ships, and ocean travel. Map a simple ocean journey.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.facebook.com/narwhalsandwaterfalls/videos/this-is-over-the-deep-blue-sea-song-we-use-in-our-ocean-explorers-storytime-les/968450392854182/

#### At the Codfish Ball
- **Artist:** Fred Penner
- **Album:** Happy Feet (1992)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Ocean life, marine community/ecosystems, fish behavior
- **Activity Description:** Fish dance—children move like fish with fin-wiggle arms and tail-wag hips. Gather into schools (groups) when music pauses. Fish tag games. Build paper plate fish and decorate them.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.musixmatch.com/lyrics/Fred-Penner/Happy-Feet

#### You Can Count On Me
- **Artist:** Fred Penner
- **Album:** Happy Feet (1992)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Counting with nature theme, fish counting
- **Activity Description:** Count along using fish counters or puppets. Combine physical counting (walking on stepping stones) with the song. Connect to beach/pool visits.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://open.spotify.com/album/4uUZmsOmjFHQv2w8s6vLkx

## KINDERGARTEN (5–6 years)

### 🐾 Animals

#### The Bear Went Over the Mountain
- **Traditional American Folk Song** (popularized by Dan Zanes, Pete Seeger, Peter, Paul and Mary)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Mountains, bear habitat, animal tracking, Appalachian ecology
- **Activity Description:** March and chant song with strong rhythmic beat. Discuss mountain ecosystems—what lives on mountains? Compare bears to other forest animals. Create bear tracks using flour dough imprints. Map major mountain ranges of North America.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; folk song tradition

#### There Was an Old Lady Who Swallowed a Fly
- **Traditional** (adapted by Pete Seeger, Raffi, Caspar Babypants)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Food chains, predator-prey, animal sizes, ecological balance
- **Activity Description:** Sequence cards showing which animal was eaten by which (fly → spider → bird → cat → dog…). Build food chain diagrams. Act out each animal movement. Discussion: does everything really get swallowed? What actually happens in nature?
- **Lyrics URL:** Traditional; general knowledge

#### Alligator
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Reptiles, alligators vs. crocodiles, swamp ecosystems, predator anatomy
- **Activity Description:** Clap-clap-jaw chomping motions with arms. Discuss where alligators live (swamps, southern US). Compare teeth types (crocodile vs. alligator). Swamp habitat diorama with clay and natural materials.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### Rattlesnake
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Desert animals, rattlesnake warnings, snake anatomy, desert biomes
- **Activity Description:** Rattle sounds with fingers or maracas. Sideways "snake walk." Discussion: Where do rattlesnakes live? How does their rattle work? Safety rules for encountering wild snakes. Snake shedding skin observation (found skins at nature centers).
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### Rockin' Robin
- **Traditional** (performed by Pete Seeger, Caspar Babypants and others)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Birds, bird calling, robin behavior, seasonal arrival of birds
- **Activity Description:** Robin hopping movements. Call-and-response bird calling. Listen to actual robin recordings outside. Build simple bird feeders to attract local birds. Feather identification exercise.
- **Lyrics URL:** Traditional; general knowledge

#### Mysterious Things
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Hidden/dormant life, seeds underground, insects in soil, hidden ecosystems
- **Activity Description:** Digging/exploration theme. Look under rocks and logs (safely) to discover hidden creatures. Dirt dig sensory bin. Observation journaling—children draw and record "mysterious things" they find outdoors.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### Underground
- **Artist:** Caspar Babypants
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Subterranean ecosystem, worms, burrowing animals, soil layers
- **Activity Description:** Crawl and wiggle through tunnels. Soil layer dissection in clear jars (sand, silt, clay, organic matter). Worm dissection videos or live worm bins. Discuss what keeps us alive (soil health).
- **Lyrics URL:** https://babypantsmusic.com/song-stories

#### Pterodactyl Song
- **Artist:** Kathy Reid-Naiman
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Prehistoric life, dinosaurs, fossils, extinction, evolution
- **Activity Description:** Flying wing motions, roaring dinosaur sounds. Create fossil impressions in clay or mud. Visit a natural history museum. Timeline activity—order dinosaurs from earliest to latest appearance.
- **Lyrics URL:** ticklesandtunes-kathy-reid-naiman PDF resources; general knowledge

#### I Have a Heart
- **Artist:** Judith Lander (performed by Fred Penner on Happy Feet)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Anatomy, human biology, heartbeat, pulse, cardiovascular system
- **Activity Description:** Hands over heart to feel heartbeat. Count pulse on wrist before and after exercise. Discuss what makes hearts beat faster. Blood circulation drawing. Healthy heart food choices discussion.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.deezer.com/en/album/432493877

### 🌧️ Weather

#### Stormy Weather / I Hear Thunder
- **Artist:** Traditional / Jack Hartmann / various (e.g., Let's Play Music program's "I Hear Thunder")
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Thunderstorm formation, lightning safety, rain cycle, sound/light speed difference
- **Activity Description:** Volume-based storm—quiet whispers for far-off thunder, loud stomps for close thunder. Flashlight + dark room for lightning demonstration (instant light vs. delayed sound—count seconds between flash and sound). Instruments for rain (shakers = drizzle, drums = heavy rain). Cloud-forming experiment in jar with ice and hot water.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.letsplaykidsmusic.com/i-hear-thunder-rainy-day-songs/

#### One Hundred Raindrops
- **Artist:** Jessica Harper
- **Album:** A Wonderful Life (referenced in Kennedy Center SWTA Feeling Hot, Cold and Wet resource)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Counting, raindrop size variation, precipitation cycle, water falling
- **Activity Description:** Count down from 100 as raindrops "fall" (slowly crouching). Small drops = tiptoes; big drops = giant steps. Collect rain in a graduated cylinder to measure precipitation. Track rainfall daily on a chart.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; referenced in Kennedy Center SWTA curriculum

#### May This Be Love / Sun Songs (Seasonal)
- **Artist:** Raffi – "Here Comes the Sun"; Sarah Pirtle – "The Sun Inside Us"
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Solar energy, warmth, seasonal sunlight changes, photosynthesis
- **Activity Description:** Face a "warm sun" (lamp or candle at a distance—never look directly at sun!) and feel warmth on skin. Discuss how plants need sunlight. Body thermometer activity—outside temperature vs. inside temperature. Shadow tracing at different times of day.
- **Lyrics URL:** Kennedy Center SWTA "Feeling Hot, Cold and Wet" curriculum guide: https://www.kennedy-center.org/globalassets/education/networks-conferences--research/research--resources/start-with-the-arts/swta-book---4-feeling-hot_cold_and_wet_accessible.pdf

### 🍂 Seasons

#### Turn, Turn, Turn (To Everything There Is a Season)
- **Artist:** Pete Seeger (original lyricist/adapter from Ecclesiastes)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Four seasons, cyclical time, natural cycles, earth orbit, equinoxes/solstices
- **Activity Description:** Four stations representing each season—children rotate through performing seasonal activities (planting seeds, fanning themselves, picking leaves, building a snowman out of pillows). Large calendar activity marking current season and predicting next. Solstice/equinox date investigation.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://riseupsinging.org/seeger/recordings/children

#### Here Comes Spring (All the Flowers Are Sleeping)
- **Artist:** Miss Carole / various traditional sources
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Spring awakening, flower blooming, waking hibernation, seasonal change
- **Activity Description:** Flower bud fingerplay—curl into a ball for sleeping flowers, bloom open arms for spring arrival. Flower coloring pages matching real blooms observed outside. Press flowers in books for later viewing. Community nature walk to document first spring signs.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; community music educators

### 🌱 Plants/Garden

#### I'm a Little Seed (Action Song / Raffi's Everything Grows variation)
- **Artist:** Raffi / adapted by Jessica Garrison and other early childhood educators
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Seed germination, plant lifecycle, roots/shoots, plant needs
- **Activity Description:** Classic "I'm a little teapot" style action song—curl small as a seed, unfold as sprout, grow tall as a plant, bloom as a flower. Paired with actual seed planting in clear cups to observe root development. Daily journal drawing of each seedling's growth stage.
- **Lyrics URL:** General knowledge; Jessica Garrison preschool resources

#### A Place in the Choir (Environmental/Plant-themed)
- **Artist:** Referenced in environmental children's music literature (journal.childrensmusic.org)
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Ecosystem roles, interdependence of plants and animals, biodiversity
- **Activity Description:** Each child represents a different plant/animal and has a unique "voice" (sound or movement). Build a choir representing a healthy ecosystem. Remove some roles and observe how the ecosystem changes—discuss interdependence.
- **Lyrics URL:** journal.childrensmusic.org/songs/environment/essay

#### Six Leg Boogey (Insect Education Song)
- **Artist:** Stan Slaughter
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Insect anatomy (six legs), arthropods vs. other creatures, insect diversity
- **Activity Description:** Count legs on insect illustrations (six for insects, eight for spiders). Classify creatures as insect/non-insect. Insect observation journals. Create six-legged monster crafts with googly eyes and pipe-cleaner legs.
- **Lyrics URL:** https://www.songsforteaching.com/environmentnature/sixlegboogey.php

### 💧 Water/Ocean/Nature Sounds

#### The Water Cycle Song / Rivers and Oceans
- **Traditional concept covered by multiple artists**
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Water cycle (evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection), rivers flowing to oceans
- **Activity Description:** Movement sequence: puddle crouch → steam up (evaporation) → cloud hug (condensation) → rain fall (precipitation) → stream to ocean (collection). Water cycle diagram labeled in children's words. Jar experiment: hot water + plastic wrap + ice = mini raincloud.
- **Lyrics URL:** Multiple educator-created versions; see songsforteaching.com/sciencesongs.htm

#### Owl (Woodland Night Sounds)
- **Artist:** Fred Penner – Woodland Sounds series
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Nocturnal animals, owl hearing, night vision, woodland ecosystems
- **Activity Description:** Soft whispering and hooting sounds. Owl head-turning flexibility challenge (how much can you turn?). Discuss why owls are awake at night. Black paper owl art with scratch marks for feather detail. Night walk observation if safe.
- **Lyrics URL:** Fred Penner – Woodland Sounds album; Facebook/SingBooksWithEmily references

#### Jungle Sounds Series
- **Artist:** Fred Penner
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Tropical ecosystems, jungle animals, animal sounds, jungle layers (canopy, understory, forest floor)
- **Activity Description:** Close eyes and identify jungle sounds. Match each sound to the correct animal. Layer activity: canopy sounds (high bird calls) → middle (monkey noises) → forest floor (rustling reptiles/great apes). Create paper mache jungle masks.
- **Lyrics URL:** SingBooksWithEmily/Facebook reference; Fred Penner album

#### Farm Sounds / Farmyard Friends
- **Artist:** Fred Penner
- **Nature/Science Concept:** Farm animals, domesticated animals, what farms produce, barn ecosystem
- **Activity Description:** Each animal gets a movement: cow sways, pig oinks with nose-wiggle, horse gallops. Chart what products each animal provides (milk, wool, eggs). Farm visit or virtual tour. Sort animals by what they produce.
- **Lyrics URL:** Fred Penner albums; SingBooksWithEmily reference

### Science Standards Alignment Notes
- **Earth Science:** Water cycle songs, weather songs, rain/thunder, rainbow/color light physics
- **Life Science:** Animal habitats, life cycles (seeds→plants, tadpoles→frogs), food chains, plant needs
- **Physical Science:** Sound volume (rain/thunder), light (rainbow/sun), movement/mechanics (butterfly flight, boat motion)
- **Ecosystems & Environment:** Biodiversity, interdependence, conservation messages embedded throughout Raffi and environmental song traditions
