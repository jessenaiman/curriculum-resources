### Movement Songs — Key Issues Found

1. **#5 Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star — Shakespeare myth propagated.** The file states "Hap Palmer adds Shakespeare verse" referring to the "Twinkle twinkle little bat" second stanza. Neither Hap Palmer nor anyone "added" it — it evolved organically.

2. **#11 Shake Your Sillies Out — Wrong artist.** Credited to The Learning Station, but the well-known version is by Karen Miller (The Kiboomers). These are competing companies with separate catalogs.

3. **#12 Wiggle Dance — Unverifiable title.** Danny Go is real, but this specific title cannot be confirmed in his published works.

4. **#14 Tony Chestnut — Non-standard/dubious title.** May describe a generic activity rather than referencing an actual published song.
