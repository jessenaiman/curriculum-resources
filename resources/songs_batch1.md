## Notes on Research Methodology

1. Website album pages provide general descriptions but no individual song details
2. Individual track pages (e.g., /track/1599038/how-d-ja-do) do not contain lyrics or descriptions
3. Song topics and actions were inferred from:
   - Traditional nursery rhyme knowledge
   - Kathy Reid-Naiman's focus on parent-child interaction
   - Album descriptions emphasizing "tickles, knee bounces, singing games"
   - The Parent-Child Mother Goose Program context
   - Standard early childhood music education practices
4. All songs are appropriate for the 0-6 age range per album descriptions
5. Many songs appear on multiple albums (e.g., "Sally Go Round the Sun" appears on both Tickles and Tunes and Timeless Tickles and Tunes)
