## 1. Greeting Songs

### Good Morning, Dear Friends
- **Actions/Movements:** Children sit in a circle. Teacher sings "Good morning, dear friends" and waves. Each child waves back. Can modify to include each child's name.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Social greetings, turn-taking, name recognition, belonging
- **Where to Find:** Traditional circle time song; YouTube (search "Good Morning Dear Friends circle time")

### Hello, Everybody (Yes, Indeed!)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Hello, everybody, yes indeed! Clap your hands to the beat." Children clap, then stomp, then wave, then say their name when prompted.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Greetings, rhythm, following directions, self-identification
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Dr. Jean Feldman versions on YouTube

### The More We Get Together
- **Actions/Movements:** Children hold hands in a circle, sway side to side. Replace names with classmates' names: "With [child's name] and [child's name] and [child's name] too!"
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Friendship, community, inclusion, name recognition
- **Where to Find:** Traditional folk song; Raffi, Sharon Lois & Bram versions

### Hello, Hello, What's Your Name?
- **Actions/Movements:** Children tap knees to a beat. When teacher points to a child, they say their name. Others repeat "Hello, [name]!"
- **Age Group:** Preschool (3–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Introductions, self-esteem, social interaction
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; also on Songs for Teaching website (songsforteaching.com)

### Rise and Shine (Good Morning Song)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Rise and shine, rise and shine, it's a beautiful day!" Children stretch arms up, wiggle fingers like sunshine, spin in place.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Morning routine, enthusiasm, body awareness
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Dr. Jean Feldman; also listed on songsforteaching.com

### Good Morning, Everyone
- **Actions/Movements:** Children wave, clap, stomp, and call out "Good morning!" to each person. Teacher can use a puppet or stuffed animal as a greeter.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Social greetings, eye contact, group participation
- **Where to Find:** Traditional circle time song

### Hello, My Friends (with Sign Language)
- **Actions/Movements:** Combine greeting words with simple ASL signs (hello, friend, love). Children repeat signs along with the words.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Inclusion, ASL basics, vocabulary, motor skills
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; various YouTube versions

### Today Is Monday
- **Actions/Movements:** Children sit in a circle. Teacher introduces the day of the week. Children repeat and can add body movements (clap on Monday, stomp on Tuesday, etc.)
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Days of the week, calendar awareness, sequencing
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Eric Carle "Today Is Monday" book companion activities

## 2. Movement Songs

### Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes
- **Actions/Movements:** Touch head, shoulders, knees, toes, eyes, ears, mouth, and nose in sequence. Speed up with each repetition.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Body parts, coordination, tempo/pace, listening skills
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhyme; widely available on YouTube

### The Wheels on the Bus
- **Actions/Movements:** Pretend to drive (arms steering), wipe windows (arms sweeping), open/close doors (clap shut), beep horn, swish wipers, etc.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Rhythm, actions vocabulary, community helpers, routines
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Raffi, Super Simple Songs versions

### If You're Happy and You Know It
- **Actions/Movements:** Clap hands, stomp feet, shout "Hooray!", do all three. Can add variations (whisper hooray, sneeze hooray).
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Emotions, body actions, self-expression, listening
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Dr. Jean Feldman, The Wiggles versions

### Hokey Pokey
- **Actions/Movements:** Put body parts in, shake them, pull them out. "Put your right hand in, put your right hand out, shake it all about!"
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Left/right awareness, body parts, following directions
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; widely available on YouTube

### The Itsy Bitsy Spider
- **Actions/Movements:** Climbing motions with fingers, rain fingers fluttering down, sun circle with arms.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Sequencing, perseverance, finger coordination
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; many YouTube versions

### If All the Raindrops
- **Actions/Movements:** "If all the raindrops were gumdrops and candy drops, oh what a rain that would be!" Reach up, wiggle fingers coming down like rain, pretend to catch and eat.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Imagination, weather concepts, vocabulary
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; often taught in preschools

### Shake My Sillies Out
- **Actions/Movements:** Shake whole body, clap hands, jump up and down, wiggle and giggle. "I gotta shake, shake, shake my sillies out!"
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Gross motor skills, energy release, emotional regulation
- **Where to Find:** Raffi song; widely available on YouTube

### The Goldfish (Let's Go Swimming)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Let's go swimming, let's go swimming, let's go swimming with the goldfish today!" Arms swim, body sways. Can add other animals.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Animal movements, rhythm, participation
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; various YouTube versions

### Jump Up, Turn Around
- **Actions/Movements:** "Jump up, turn around, touch the ground. Give yourself a little jump-around!" Follow instructions with whole body.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Following directions, gross motor, spatial awareness
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; also listed on songsforteaching.com

### I'm a Little Snowman / I'm a Little Teapot
- **Actions/Movements:** Full body actions — hands on hips for teapot, arms for spout/handle. Snowman version: hat on head, scarf around neck, buttons on tummy.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Body awareness, seasonal concepts, self-expression
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhymes

### Open, Shut Them
- **Actions/Movements:** Open and close hands, hide them behind back. "Open, shut them, open, shut them, give a little clap. Open, shut them, open, shut them, lay them in your lap."
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (1–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Fine motor, following directions, calming strategy
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; very commonly used in circle time

### Walking, Walking (Super Simple Songs)
- **Actions/Movements:** Walk, tiptoe, run, stop. "Walking, walking, walking, walking. Hop, hop, hop! Running, running, running. Now let's stop!"
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Action words, self-regulation, stopping on cue
- **Where to Find:** Super Simple Songs (YouTube)

### Skidamarink (I Love You)
- **Actions/Movements:** Point to eyes ("I"), heart ("love"), and child ("you"). Add gestures for "skidamarink a dink a dink" (wiggle fingers).
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Expressing love, affection, social bonds
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; many YouTube versions

### Pat-a-Cake
- **Actions/Movements:** Clap own hands, then clap partner's hands. Roll hands ("roll it"), make a letter with finger ("mark it with B").
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (1–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Letter recognition, partner interaction, rhythm
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhyme

### Row, Row, Row Your Boat
- **Actions/Movements:** Hold hands in circle, rock forward and back. "Gently down the stream" — sway side to side. Can do as a round.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Cooperation, rhythm, circle formations
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Raffi, Sharon Lois & Bram versions

## 3. Fingerplays

### Five Little Monkeys
- **Actions/Movements:** Hold up five fingers. One by one, fold a finger down as each monkey "falls." Wag finger for "no more monkeys!"
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Counting backward (5-4-3-2-1), subtraction, memory
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; many YouTube versions

### Five Little Ducks
- **Actions/Movements:** Hold up five fingers. One by one fold fingers as ducks go away. Big mama duck calls "quack quack quack!" Flip hand back and forth.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Counting, subtraction, cause and effect, persistence
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Raffi, Super Simple Songs versions

### Two Little Blackbirds (Two Little Speckled Frogs)
- **Actions/Movements:** Two fingers (like birds) on each hand. Fly one away, then the other. "Come back!" Fly them back. Frog version: sit on a log, one jumps in!
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Counting to two, directions (away/back), memory
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhymes

### Where Is Thumbkin?
- **Actions/Movements:** Hide hands behind back with thumbs tucked. Bring out one at a time, wiggle thumbs, hide again.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Finger isolation, left/right, hide and seek
- **Where to Find:** Traditional fingerplay

### This Little Piggy
- **Actions/Movements:** Wiggle each toe as you name it. "This little piggy went to market..." End with tickle: "Wee wee wee all the way home!"
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (1–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Body parts (toes), anticipation, touch
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhyme

### The Finger Family
- **Actions/Movements:** Each finger gets a family member: "Daddy finger, daddy finger, where are you? Here I am!" (wiggle thumb). Repeat for mommy, brother, sister, baby.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Family members, finger isolation, roles
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; many YouTube versions

### One, Two, Buckle My Shoe
- **Actions/Movements:** Count on fingers. "One, two — buckle my shoe" (mime buckling). Continue through ten.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Counting, rhyming, sequencing to 10
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhyme

### Tommy Thumb (Where Is Thumbkin?)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Tommy Thumb, Tommy Thumb, where are you?" Wiggle thumb behind back. "Here I am, here I am!" Repeat with pointer, middle, ring, and baby finger.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Finger names, fine motor, hide and seek concept
- **Where to Find:** Traditional fingerplay

### A Little Turtle
- **Actions/Movements:** Fist is turtle shell. Poke head (index finger) out. "I met a little turtle as I walked along the way. He poked his head out, looked around, and pulled it in to stay!"
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Animal behavior, fine motor, cause and effect
- **Where to Find:** Traditional fingerplay

### Five Green and Speckled Frogs
- **Actions/Movements:** Five fingers on a log (arm). One frog jumps in (fold finger). "One jumped into the pool where it was nice and cool!" Count remaining.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Counting backward, subtraction, frog facts
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; many YouTube versions

### Incy Wincy Spider (Itsy Bitsy Spider)
- **Actions/Movements:** Climb fingers up, then spread apart for rain. Climb back up again. "Down came the rain and washed the spider out..."
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Sequencing, perseverance, fine motor
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhyme

### Round and Round the Garden
- **Actions/Movements:** Trace circle on child's palm. "Round and round the garden, goes the little bear. One step, two step, and tickle you under there!" Tickle at the end.
- **Age Group:** Babies–Preschool (6 months–3 years)
- **Teaches:** Anticipation, touch, trust, circle concept
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; very commonly used in early years

### 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 — Once I Caught a Fish Alive
- **Actions/Movements:** Count on fingers. "I bit my finger so hard I cried!" (hold up finger). Switch to counting 6-7-8-9-10. "I'll never do that again!"
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Counting to 10, rhyming, cause and effect
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhyme

### Peter Piper
- **Actions/Movements:** "Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers" — mime picking and placing. Repeat faster each time.
- **Age Group:** Kindergarten (4–6 years)
- **Teaches:** Tongue twisters, alliteration, speech clarity
- **Where to Find:** Traditional nursery rhyme

## 4. Transition Songs

### Clean-Up Song
- **Actions/Movements:** "Clean up, clean up, everybody everywhere! Clean up, clean up, everybody do your share!" Children move to pick up toys and put them away.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Responsibility, tidying, cooperation, routine
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Barney version; also "Tidy Up" by Dr. Jean on songsforteaching.com

### The Cleanup Song (by Jack Hartmann)
- **Actions/Movements:** "It's cleanup time, cleanup time!" Children find toys, put them in bins. "Cleanup Vacuum Cleaner" — children roll across floor picking things up.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Responsibility, cleanup routine, cooperation
- **Where to Find:** Jack Hartmann on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Tidy Up (by Dr. Jean Feldman)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Tidy up, tidy up, tidy up now!" Children put toys away while singing. Can add a timer element.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Clean-up routine, responsibility, time management
- **Where to Find:** Dr. Jean on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Time to Line Up
- **Actions/Movements:** "Time to line up, time to line up, like little soldiers, standing tall!" Children form a line, hands at sides or behind back.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Following directions, lining up, self-regulation
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; also "Line-Up Cadence" by Dr. Jean on songsforteaching.com

### The Lining-Up Song (by Jack Hartmann)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Walk in line, walk in line, keep your hands to yourself!" Children practice walking in a straight line.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Self-control, spatial awareness, safety
- **Where to Find:** Jack Hartmann on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Criss Cross Applesauce
- **Actions/Movements:** "Criss cross applesauce, spiders crawling up the back. Up the back, up the back, spiders crawling up the back — CRACK!" Children sit cross-legged and tap backs.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Sitting position, listening, anticipation
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Stephen Fite version on songsforteaching.com

### Wash Your Hands (by Miss Jenny)
- **Actions/Movements:** Follow hand-washing steps while singing: water, soap, scrub, rinse, dry. "Scrub, scrub, scrub, scrub, wash your hands with me!"
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Hygiene, self-care, sequencing steps
- **Where to Find:** Miss Jenny on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### The Germ Song / Hand Washing Song
- **Actions/Movements:** "Germs, germs, go away! Wash your hands throughout the day!" Mime scrubbing between fingers, under nails.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Hygiene, health, proper hand-washing technique
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Music with Mar.; songsforteaching.com

### Snack Time Song
- **Actions/Movements:** "It's snack time, snack time, who's ready for snack time?" Children wash hands, sit at tables, practice manners.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Mealtime routine, manners, patience
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; many daycare adaptations

### It's Time to Go (by Hap Palmer)
- **Actions/Movements:** "It's time to go, it's time to go, put on your shoes, it's time to go!" Children follow along with actions.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Transition awareness, self-preparation
- **Where to Find:** Hap Palmer; songsforteaching.com

### Circle Time is Here (by Stephen Fite)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Circle time is here, come sit down!" Children move to circle area and sit cross-legged.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Routine, transitioning to group activities
- **Where to Find:** Stephen Fite on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Come and Make a Circle (by Susan Salidor)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Come and make a circle, come and make a circle, come and make a circle, round and round!" Children join hands and form a circle.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Group formation, cooperation, routines
- **Where to Find:** Susan Salidor on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### The Quietest Line (by Miss Jenny)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Can you walk the quietest line?" Children practice silent walking in a line.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Self-regulation, volume control, safety
- **Where to Find:** Miss Jenny on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Transition Counting Songs
- **Actions/Movements:** "1, 2, 3, eyes on me!" or "1, 2, 3, look at me!" Teacher calls for attention. Children respond "4, 5, 6, we are watching!" or similar.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Counting, attention-getting, self-regulation
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; various teacher adaptations

### Magic Words (Please and Thank You)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Magic words are important, magic words are kind. Please and thank you make the world work right — they help us all to shine!"
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Manners, polite language, social skills
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; various teacher adaptations

## 5. Goodbye Songs

### The Goodbye Song
- **Actions/Movements:** "Goodbye, goodbye, we'll see you soon! Goodbye, goodbye, it's time to go!" Wave to each child by name. Can add "see you tomorrow, see you next time."
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (1–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Farewell routine, closure, social bonds
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; many YouTube versions; songsforteaching.com

### So Long, Farewell (from The Sound of Music)
- **Actions/Movements:** Children wave, bow, curtsy as they sing. "So long, farewell, auf Wiedersehen, goodnight!"
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (4–6 years)
- **Teaches:** Goodbye phrases in multiple languages, formal farewells
- **Where to Find:** The Sound of Music soundtrack; also Stephen Fite version on songsforteaching.com

### See You Later, Alligator
- **Actions/Movements:** "See you later, alligator! After a while, crocodile!" Wave and walk toward the door. Can add more animal goodbyes.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Rhyming, animal names, farewell expressions
- **Where to Find:** Traditional; Jack Hartmann version on songsforteaching.com

### Goodbye, My Friends
- **Actions/Movements:** "Goodbye, my friends, goodbye! I'm so glad we played together!" Hug self, wave, blow kisses.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Emotional closure, friendship, routine
- **Where to Find:** Traditional circle time song

### Time to Say Goodbye (by Pam Donkin)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Time to say goodbye, time to wave and say so long!" Children wave, stand, and prepare to leave.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Closure, transition to home routine
- **Where to Find:** Pam Donkin on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Going Home (by Music with Mar.)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Going home, going home, it's time to go home now!" Children gather belongings and line up.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** End-of-day routine, independence (getting belongings)
- **Where to Find:** Music with Mar. on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Goodbye to You (by Music with Mar.)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Goodbye to you, goodbye to you, we've had fun today!" Wave to each child. Child responds "Goodbye!" and leaves circle.
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Kindergarten (2–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Individual acknowledgment, closure, social skills
- **Where to Find:** Music with Mar. on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### The Goodbye Song (by Cathy Bollinger)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Goodbye, goodbye, it's time to say goodbye!" Wave arms, hug self, high-five teacher.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Farewell routine, self-regulation at end of day
- **Where to Find:** Cathy Bollinger on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Wave Goodbye (by Colleen and Uncle Squaty)
- **Actions/Movements:** Wave big waves, little waves, slow waves, fast waves. "Wave goodbye, wave goodbye, wave goodbye to all our friends!"
- **Age Group:** Toddlers–Preschool (2–4 years)
- **Teaches:** Farewell, motor skills, variations
- **Where to Find:** Colleen and Uncle Squaty on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Good-Bye Means "God Be With You" (by Jim Rule)
- **Actions/Movements:** Gentle waving, hand on heart. "Goodbye means God be with you, until we meet again."
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** Etymology, farewell meaning, gentle closure
- **Where to Find:** Jim Rule on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Farewell a la Pachelbel (Round) (by Jim Rule)
- **Actions/Movements:** Sing in rounds. Children wave gently as they sing.
- **Age Group:** Kindergarten (4–6 years)
- **Teaches:** Round singing, classical music exposure, gentle farewell
- **Where to Find:** Jim Rule on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

### Back Pack Boogie (by Dr. Jean Feldman)
- **Actions/Movements:** "Pack up your backpack, put on your shoes, it's time to go, it's time to go!" Children follow along with getting-ready actions.
- **Age Group:** Preschool–Kindergarten (3–5 years)
- **Teaches:** End-of-day routine, self-care, independence
- **Where to Find:** Dr. Jean on YouTube; songsforteaching.com

## Tips for Using Circle Time Songs

1. **Be consistent** — Use the same songs daily so children learn the routine
2. **Keep it short** — Circle time should be 10-20 minutes for preschoolers
3. **Include everyone** — Use songs where children can participate at their level
4. **Add instruments** — Shakers, drums, or scarves make songs more engaging
5. **Repeat favorites** — Children learn through repetition; don't rush to new songs
6. **Match energy levels** — Start with movement songs, transition to calmer fingerplays
7. **Use visuals** — Picture cards or puppets help non-verbal children participate
8. **Adapt for abilities** — Modify songs for children with different needs
9. **Transition gradually** — Use "clean up" songs BEFORE you need them
10. **Make it fun!** — Enthusiasm is contagious; your energy sets the tone
