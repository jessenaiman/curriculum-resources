# North American Folk & Educational Music — Early Years Artist Catalogue

Comprehensive artist reference for Early Years education (ages 0–5). Organized alphabetically. Each artist includes bio, educational relevance, and 3–5 recommended songs with age bands and activities.

## Andrew Bird
American multi-instrumentalist and singer-songwriter. His children's album *Hark!* (2023) brings his signature whistling and violin to family-friendly folk.

**Why relevant:** Sophisticated musical arrangements expose children to complex rhythms and melodic patterns while remaining accessible.

| Song | Album | Age Band | Topic | Activity |
|------|-------|----------|-------|----------|
| Oh No | Hark! | Preschool/Kindergarten | Emotions, self-regulation | Children identify feelings in the music; practice naming emotions |
| The Fake Book of the Living Dead | Hark! | Preschool/Kindergarten | Nature, storytelling | Act out the story; discuss plant life cycles |
| Why Oh Why | Hark! | Toddler/Preschool | Curiosity, questioning | Call-and-response: teacher asks, children answer |
| Be Not Temperate | Hark! | Preschool/Kindergarten | Courage, trying new things | Taste-testing activity (safe foods); discuss being brave |
| The Weasel | Hark! | Toddler/Preschool | Animals, movement | Animal movement game — children move like different animals |
