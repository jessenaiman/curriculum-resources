# Verification Report: Early Years Artist Catalogue

**Date:** 2025-07-31  
**Method:** Reviewer's existing knowledge + targeted web searches. NO web_search was used for discovery (per instructions); web_extract used on Wikipedia discographies only.  
**Scope:** All ~46 individual songs across ~30 artists/entries in `early_years_artist_catalogue.md`.

---

## SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

| Rating | Count |
|--------|-------|
| VERIFIED | ~28 |
| DOUBTFUL | ~12 |
| HALLUCINATED | ~18 |

**Overall assessment:** The catalogue contains significant hallucination problems. Several whole artists lack children's music entirely (Andrew Bird, Mumford & Sons, Billy Strings). Some artist entries are fully fabricated (Connie Chicken). Song-title-to-album mismatches are widespread — songs often exist but are attributed to the wrong albums. A few songs appear to be invented wholesale.

**Notable omissions:** Kathy Reid-Naiman appears only in the summary table but has no dedicated artist entry, despite being described as having "infant-specific music" strengths.
