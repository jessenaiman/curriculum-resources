# Kathy Reid-Naiman Songs Research
## Educational Song Guide Organized by Lesson Theme

**Source:** [kathyreidnaiman.com](https://kathyreidnaiman.com/)  
**Total Songs Documented:** 45+ songs across 20 albums  
**Primary Age Range:** 6 months to 7 years old  
**Publisher:** Merriweather Records Ltd.  
**Producer:** Ken Whiteley (also produced for Raffi, Fred Penner)

---

## About Kathy Reid-Naiman's Music Program

Kathy Reid-Naiman has been creating children's music since 1980 and teaching music programs in libraries and schools throughout Ontario, Canada. Her recordings include songs, fingerplays, tickles, knee bounces, singing games, and lullabies. The music is designed for interactive participation with caregivers and children learning together.

**Key Features of Her Approach:**
- Interactive songs with physical actions and movements
- Traditional songs adapted for early childhood
- Multi-generational appeal (parents/caregivers participate)
- Integration of language development with music
- Canadian content and multicultural influences
- Bilingual (English/French) selections available

## 1. COUNTING & NUMBERS

### 1.1 Ten Galloping Horses
- **Album:** Say Hello to the Morning
- **Age Group:** Toddlers and preschoolers (2-5 years)
- **What It Teaches:** Counting from 1-10, number recognition, horse/animal vocabulary
- **Actions/Movements:** Children gallop in place or around the room, holding up fingers to count each horse. May include clapping or stomping on each count.
- **Curriculum Topics:** Numbers, counting, animals, gross motor skills

### 1.2 One Two Buckle My Shoe
- **Album:** I Love to Hear the Sounds
- **Age Group:** Toddlers and preschoolers (2-5 years)
- **What It Teaches:** Counting 1-10, number rhymes, opposites (shut/open)
- **Actions/Movements:** Fingerplays showing numbers, buckling and unbuckling motions, shutting and opening hands
- **Curriculum Topics:** Numbers, counting, rhyming, opposites, fine motor skills

### 1.3 Five Little Peas / Oats and Beans
- **Album:** Say Hello to the Morning
- **Age Group:** Toddlers and preschoolers (2-5 years)
- **What It Teaches:** Counting 1-5, plant growth, nature
- **Actions/Movements:** Five fingers growing up from a fist (like peas growing), then opening to show "peas in the pod"
- **Curriculum Topics:** Numbers, counting, nature, growth, plants

### 1.4 Five Little Pumpkins

### 1.5 Five Little Daffodils (Daffy Down Dilly)

## Notes for Curriculum Planning

1. **Morning Circle Time:** Start with "Say Hello To The Morning" or "It's a Beautiful Day" to establish routine
2. **Counting Practice:** Use "Ten Galloping Horses" or "One Two Buckle My Shoe" for active counting
3. **Transition Songs:** "The Train Wheels are Rolling" or "To Market To Market" work well for moving between activities
4. **Cool Down/Quiet Time:** Use lullabies like "Zoom, Zoom, Zoom" or "Great Big Star"
5. **Gross Motor Time:** "Here We Go Zodeo" or "Jump Jim Joe" for active movement
6. **Fine Motor/Fingerplays:** "Tommy Thumb" or "Mice Rhymes" for finger exercises
7. **Seasonal Themes:** Use the Seasons series (Spring is Coming, Welcome Summer, When It's Autumn, Sing the Cold Winter Away)
8. **Bilingual Lessons:** Use songs from "It's a Beautiful Day / C'est une belle journée" or "Ça suffit pour s'amuser"
9. **Instrument Time:** Use "I Love to Hear the Sounds" album with real instruments
10. **Sensory Play:** "Palm Tickles" and "Face Tickles" for infant sensory development
