## BACKLOG (Not Started)

### R-001: Pat Pattison's Prosody Techniques — Deep Dive
- **Why:** Pattison is the definitive voice on prosody. Need his specific exercises, examples, and the distinction between "appropriate" and "perfect" prosody.
- **Deliverable:** 10-15 concrete examples of prosody working (and failing) in real songs, with scansion notation.
- **Sources:** Berklee lectures (YouTube), *Writing Better Lyrics*, *Songwriting: Essential Guide to Lyric Form and Structure*

### R-002: Max Martin Song Breakdowns — Melodic Math in Action
- **Why:** We have the theory but no worked examples. Need 5-8 specific songs analyzed showing Prince Theory, melodic previews, glue hooks, growing climaxes.
- **Deliverable:** Annotated breakdowns of: "…Baby One More Time," "Teenage Dream," "Save Your Tears," "Since U Been Gone," "I Want It That Way," "Shake It Off," "Blinding Lights"
- **Sources:** Top40 Theory (top40theory.com), Hit Songs Deconstructed, YouTube breakdowns

### R-003: Vowel-First Lyric Writing — The ABBA/Martin Technique
- **Why:** "They optimize for how the words feel in the mouth, not just what they mean." This is the missing link between prosody and hook engineering.
- **Deliverable:** Analysis of how ABBA and Martin use vowel sounds to create emotional impact, with practical writing exercises.
- **Sources:** Songwriting Academy, interviews with Björn Ulvaeus, Asaf Peres analysis

### R-004: Recursive Composition — Repetition with Variation
- **Why:** The Theory notes describe "recursive storytelling" as a core technique. Need to understand how this works across an entire song — how motifs evolve, gain new meaning, and reward repeated listening.
- **Deliverable:** 3-4 song analyses showing recursive structure (Leonard Cohen "Hallelujah," Radiohead "Creep," Beatles "Let It Be," or similar)
- **Sources:** Songwriting craft blogs, music theory analysis, Pat Pattison on motif development

### R-005: Symbolic Density — Writing on Two Levels
- **Why:** "Every verse should function on at least two levels." Need to understand how great lyricists embed hidden meaning without being obscure.
- **Deliverable:** Analysis of songs with high symbolic density — how the surface narrative works AND how the deeper meaning emerges through repetition.
- **Sources:** Leonard Cohen, Bob Dylan, Joni Mitchell, Radiohead analyses

### R-006: Odd Time Signatures in Practice
- **Why:** The Theory notes describe 5/4, 7/8, 6/8 as creative tools. Need real examples of how these feel in practice, not just theory.
- **Deliverable:** 5-6 song examples of odd meters with analysis of how the lyrics fit the rhythm.
- **Sources:** Dave Brubeck "Take Five," Radiohead "15 Step," Mission Impossible theme, Björk, progressive rock analyses
